# S13. Idea mode, Low

> A new mechanic, not a restyle. The question set is generated, and answering one question can
> rewrite the questions that come later. Everything here was decided on 18 September.

> **Where the ids come from.** Every `C`, `E`, `A` and `K` id below lives in its register:
> `14-COMPONENT-INVENTORY.md`, `17-ERROR-AND-REFUSAL-CATALOGUE.md`, `19-ACCEPTANCE-CRITERIA.md` and
> `16-COPY-DECK.md`. The ids this screen first proposed were reconciled into those registers on 18
> September 2026. The old-to-new mapping is in `tools/error-map.json`, `tools/component-map.json`
> and `tools/acceptance-map.json`, and `tools/validate-pack.py` fails if any id here has no home.

## Purpose

Ask a person the ten to fifteen questions that actually decide their product, four at a time, and
change the later questions when an earlier answer makes them wrong.

## The mechanic, in the order it happens

This is the part a builder most needs and it is spread across three documents, so it is gathered
here once.

Step | What happens | Where it was decided
1 | The person sends the idea on S12 | `docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:110`
2 | **The whole question set is generated once, in one call**, from the idea | `:348`
3 | The count is 10 to 15, set by how complex the idea is | `:111`
4 | Page one renders **with everything already planned**, four questions at most | `:112`, `:349`
5 | Each question carries a **branching flag**, set when it was generated | `:350`
6 | An answer to a non-branching question turns the page instantly, and costs nothing | `:351`
7 | An answer to a branching question **rewrites the later pages** | `:113`
8 | Only then does the affected card blur and say why, naming the answer that caused it | `:115`, `:353`
9 | Rewrites are capped at **three per blueprint on Free**, unbounded on Pro | `:355`
10 | The fifteen blueprint files are listed before the credit is spent | `docs/mvp0/SCREENS.md:167`

**Most page turns are instant and free.** That is the design, and it is why the blur means
something when a person does see it.

### What it costs, from the plan's own costing

`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:332` onward carries the costing, against a Low
baseline of 15 calls, 81,825 tokens in and 31,365 out. **These are simulated, not measured**, and
the baseline itself is assumed until measured.

Rewrites | Extra tokens | Extra cost on a free model | 200 free users a month
0 | 1,500 in, 2,500 out | $0.0009 | $3.12
3 | 10,500 in, 7,000 out | $0.0029 | $3.51
4 | 13,500 in, 8,500 out | $0.0035 | $3.64

The conclusion recorded there: dynamic questioning costs about three tenths of a cent per
blueprint, so **it is not a Pro feature**, because the free product is the funnel and a paywalled
question set would make the free product ask worse questions.

**The real cost is latency and request count** against per-organisation free pools, not money. The
cap of three exists for that and for the abuse surface, not for the cents.

### The question card

Element | Rule
The question | numbered across the whole set, so question 7 is question 7 on page two
The options | a key letter and the option text
**The reason** | **only the recommended option carries one**. Not every option gets a justification, because that turns a choice into a wall of text
The recommended tag | on one option, once
Not sure | a quiet link under the options, **never a third option in the list**

- **A recommendation is never a percentage.** The plan's section 9 takes this from the guidance that
  confidence should not be shown when it is not impactful.
- **At Low, the screen may never show evidence it did not read.** That is the plan's honesty rule
  per depth, and Low is the depth with no retrieval and no research pass behind it.

### Not sure, and what it writes

This is the most easily got wrong behaviour on the screen, so it is stated as three separate facts.

Fact | Detail
It records the question as **open** | not as answered, and not as skipped
It takes the recommendation **for now** | so the blueprint is complete and buildable
`DECISIONS.md` carries it as **open** | not as decided, so the kit tells the truth about what nobody chose

### The rewrite

The blur is information, so it only appears when there is something to say.

Condition | What the person sees
A non-branching answer | nothing; the page turns
A branching answer, within the cap | the affected cards blur, and a line names the answer that caused it
A branching answer, at the cap on Free | no rewrite, and `E077` says the later questions stay as planned
A rewrite that fails | `E086`, and the previously planned questions stand

- The line names the cause: which answer, and which pages are being rewritten.
- **Only the affected cards blur**, not the page and not the screen. A person can still read the
  questions that are not changing.
- The rewrite never changes a question already answered.

### Skip all, and the modal

`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:120` to `:124` sets this out exactly.

Page | What `C108` carries
One | Skip, Choose the recommendation, Next
Two onward | the same three, **plus Skip all remaining**

`C109` says three things plainly, and it is not dismissible by clicking away:

1. The recommended option is chosen for every remaining question.
2. The plan is generated from those defaults.
3. The kickoff prompt follows the same defaults.

**Skip all is never silent.** It is the one control that decides the rest of the product on a
person's behalf, so it says so before it does it.

### The standard set toggle, inverted

`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:359` inverted the founders' suggested toggle.

- **Dynamic is the default**, for everyone, on Free.
- The toggle reads *use a standard question set*.
- It is the **graceful-degradation path** when the model layer is degraded or the person is over
  their cap.
- That turns it from a paywall into a fallback, and it gives S32 something useful to offer instead
  of an apology.

## Data contract

What | Direction | Port or store | Shape | When
The question set | in | generated on S12's send, stored on the idea | questions with options, a recommendation, a reason, and a **branching flag** | once, on send
Answers | out | `IdeaWriter.answer(ideaId, questionId, value)` | answered, skipped, or open | on every choice
A rewrite | out | `LlmClient.generate()` | the later questions, regenerated | only on a branching answer, within the cap
Rewrite count | in and out | the idea record | an integer against the cap | incremented per rewrite
`DECISIONS.md` | out | `DocumentWriter.splice()` | one heading per decision, with an `open` marker for Not sure | on completion
Blueprint credit | out | `LedgerWriter.append()` | one blueprint credit | **after `C111` is shown**, never before
The file list | in | the template's file list | fifteen names | before the credit

- **`DECISIONS.md` carries its own version field, `decisions: 1` in front matter**, per the plan's
  section 20, with unknown fields ignored and a fixture as its test.
- **The branching flag is generated with the question**, not inferred later. A question that was not
  marked as branching never triggers a rewrite, however surprising the answer.
- The whole set lives on the idea record, so a person who closes the tab returns to the same
  questions.

## States

State | When | What is shown | What the person can do
first-run | the first idea on this account | page one, plus one line on what Skip and Not sure do | answer, skip, or take the recommendations
empty | not reachable | n/a; a set exists before this screen opens | n/a
loading | only on a rewrite | the affected cards blurred, the rest readable | read the unaffected cards, or wait
partial | some questions answered | the progress line's count, and the answered cards marked | continue from where the page is
offline | no network | answering works and is stored locally; a branching answer does not rewrite and says so | keep answering; the set stands
unauthorised | the session expired | routes to S01, answers kept locally | sign in; nothing is lost
over-cap | at three rewrites on Free | answering works; `E077` says later questions stay as planned | continue, or move to Pro
AI-unavailable | the chain is down | `C072` is offered, and the planned set still works | use the standard set, or continue with what is planned
conflict | n/a | n/a; the set belongs to one idea and one person | n/a
error | a rewrite failed | `E086`, and the previously planned questions stand | continue; nothing was lost
degraded | the pool is exhausted | `E082`, with the same fallback as AI-unavailable | continue, or use the standard set

**No failure on this screen loses an answer.** Every failure path leaves the planned set in place
and lets the person carry on, because the set was generated once and is held on the idea.

## Keyboard and focus

- On arrival focus is on the first unanswered option, so a person can answer without reaching for a
  pointer.
- The key letters shown on each option are live: pressing `a` or `b` chooses that option.
- Up and down move between options; Tab moves between question cards.
- Enter on the navigation's primary control turns the page.
- **Escape does not leave the screen.** The answers are stored, so there is nothing to abandon, and
  a stray Escape mid-flow should not feel like losing work.
- `C109` is a focus trap with the confirmation focused last, not first.

## Responsive

- One column, full width. The layout was already one column, which is why the phone needs no second
  design.
- **Two question cards a page at phone width**, not four. The founders' maximum of four is a
  maximum, and four cards on a phone is a scroll nobody finishes.
- The progress line shortens to the page position, the bar and `C105`.
- The navigation becomes two controls, Skip and the recommendation, with Next as the primary and
  Skip all in the overflow from page two.
- The blur state is unchanged, because it is the thing that explains what just happened.

## Desktop differences

- Medium and High run on a local model, free, with nothing leaving the machine. At Low the desktop
  behaves as the web does.
- **The rewrite cap does not apply on the desktop when a local model is running it**, because the
  cap exists for the shared free pool.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never generate the set page by page.** One call, on send, is the decision and the cost model.
- **Never fire a rewrite on a non-branching answer.**
- **Never blur when nothing is being rewritten.** A constant processing state is noise, and then the
  real one means nothing.
- **Never rewrite a question that has already been answered.**
- **Never exceed three rewrites per blueprint on Free.**
- **Never make dynamic questioning a Pro feature.** The free product is the funnel, and the costing
  says it is three tenths of a cent.
- **Never run Skip all without the modal**, and never let the modal be dismissed by clicking away.
- **Never write Not sure into `DECISIONS.md` as a decision.**
- **Never give every option a reason.** Only the recommended one carries one.
- **Never show a recommendation as a percentage or a confidence score.**
- **Never show evidence at Low that the run did not read.**
- **Never spend a blueprint credit before the fifteen files have been listed.**
- **Never lose an answer on any failure path.**
- **Never use an emoji on an option, a tag or the navigation.**
