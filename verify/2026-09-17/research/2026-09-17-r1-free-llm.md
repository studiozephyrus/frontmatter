## B. Routing the three tasks, and where each free pool runs out

Assumption: a kit is 11 calls (one per file): 60,000 / 11 = 5,455 in and 23,000 / 11 = 2,091 out per call, 7,546 tokens a call. Edit = 4,800 tokens a call, document = 3,500, kit = 83,000.

1. **Rewrite a selection:** Groq openai/gpt-oss-120b (fastest, no retention, no training). Runs out at 200,000 TPD / 4,800 = 41 edits a day for the whole organisation, and 8,000 TPM / 4,800 = 1.67, so about one edit a minute; a second user in the same minute waits. Overflow to Cloudflare @cf/qwen/qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8: 4,000 x 4,625 / 1e6 + 800 x 30,475 / 1e6 = 18.5 + 24.4 = 42.9 neurons, 10,000 / 42.9 = 233 edits a day (gpt-oss-120b on Cloudflare: 127.3 + 54.5 = 181.8 neurons, 55 a day).
2. **Generate a document:** Cloudflare qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8: 2,000 x 4,625 / 1e6 + 1,500 x 30,475 / 1e6 = 9.3 + 45.7 = 55.0 neurons, 10,000 / 55.0 = 181 documents a day. Groq second: 200,000 / 3,500 = 57 a day, shared with edits. Gemini free would be the obvious choice, but its daily limit is not published and it trains.
3. **The kit:** Cerebras gpt-oss-120b trial: 1,000,000 TPD / 83,000 = 12 kits a day; 5 RPM makes one kit at least 11 / 5 = 2.2 minutes; the $5 credit at $0.35 and $0.75 buys 60,000 x 0.35 / 1e6 + 23,000 x 0.75 / 1e6 = $0.021 + $0.01725 = $0.0383 a kit, so 130 kits, then it stops (30 days at most). After that: OpenRouter nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free (Nvidia endpoint): 50 / 11 = 4 kits a day, or 1,000 / 11 = 90 a day once $10 of credits has ever been bought; Cloudflare qwen3-30b: 277.5 + 700.9 = 978 neurons, 10 kits a day; Groq: 200,000 / 83,000 = 2 kits a day, and 8K TPM makes each one at least 11 minutes; SambaNova: 20 requests a day, 1 kit.

## C. Free tiers that forbid production or train on prompts

- **Gemini API unpaid quota:** trains, human review, "Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the Unpaid Services". Same for OpenRouter :free endpoints served by "Google AI Studio" (the gemma-4 ones).
- **Mistral Free plan:** the training row shows a check mark with no opt-out (my reading), and Labs or Preview models train regardless. Use only after upgrading or after Studio shows an opt-out.
- **SambaNova preview models** gemma-4-31B-it and DeepSeek-V3.2: "should not be used in production environments" (no training clause).
- **NVIDIA free endpoints:** "development and testing"; data terms not found on any opened page, so unknown.
- **OpenRouter generally:** the provider's terms govern; "Where disclosed to us, we label Models that do not use your data for training". Pick endpoints by provider.
- **GitHub Models:** retired.

Clean by clause: Groq, Cerebras, Cloudflare, Together (opt-in), HF routing, SambaNova production models, Anthropic paid, local Ollama.

## D. Fallback order on a 429

1. Local Ollama in the desktop build for edits: no rate limit, nothing leaves the machine.
2. Groq: fastest, no training, retry-after header; the 8K TPM trips first.
3. Cloudflare Workers AI: 10,000 neurons a day, 300 RPM, no training; overage at "$0.011 / 1,000 Neurons" is the cheapest paid path without adding a provider.
4. Cerebras trial: largest daily token pool, but 5 RPM and a 30-day clock.
5. OpenRouter :free on Nvidia-served endpoints: 20 RPM and 50 a day is small; the 429 carries X-RateLimit-Reset.
6. SambaNova free: 20 requests a day, a smoke test only.

Never in the free chain: Gemini unpaid, Mistral Free, GitHub. Paid escape hatch: Groq developer plan (gpt-oss-120b "1K | 500K | 250K" RPM, RPD, TPM) or Anthropic Haiku 4.5. Order: no-training clause, then requests per minute headroom, then daily cap, then retry signalling.
