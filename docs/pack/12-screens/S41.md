# S41. Voice

> **Where the ids come from.** Every `C` id below already lives in `14-COMPONENT-INVENTORY.md`. An
> id written `new:` plus a slug is a proposal. None of them is in a register yet, and "Register rows
> needed" at the foot lists every one for its owner. The `new:voice-*` error ids and the event names
> are the ones `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` sections 11 and 17.4 already propose; this file reuses them rather
> than inventing a second set.

> **What this screen draws.** The speech chain, the prompts, the checks, the caps and the privacy
> rules belong to `71-VOICE-SPEC.md`. This file says only how a person meets them. Where the two
> disagree, `71` wins and this file is the one to fix.

## Purpose

Speak into my document instead of typing, get clean text I can accept with one key, and say a short
instruction such as "make this a list" without it changing anything until I agree.

## The rules this screen carries

- **The microphone is live only while the key is held**, or while toggle mode is on. The pill is on
  screen for exactly that long (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 6.2).
- **Cleaned-up words are a pending block, never pasted in.** The raw transcript appears at once, the
  restructured text replaces it while still pending, `Tab` accepts and `Esc` swaps to the raw words
  (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 8.2). It never accepts itself on a timer.
- **A command is a proposal.** The spoken words are not inserted, the proposal carries Insert as text
  instead, and nothing in the file moves before Accept (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 7.3).
- **Tone applies at High only**, so the pill and the settings show it greyed at Low and Medium
  (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 5.2).
- **Audio is never stored**, and the settings group says so in one line (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 13).
- **English only**, shown as a fixed row so the limit is visible (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 9).

## Entry and exit

Direction | How | Route or control
In | Hold `Cmd/Ctrl + .` in an open document | The voice key, `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 6.1
In | Press `Cmd/Ctrl + .` once, in toggle mode | The same key, `voice.keyMode = toggle`
In | Hold `Alt` or `Option` as well, to force a command | Stage 0 of `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 7.1
In, phone | Tap the mic in the bottom bar | `new:voice-bar-mic`
In, settings | Settings, the Voice section | `C141`, then the Voice group
Out | Release the key, or press it again in toggle mode | The turn ends; the pending block stays
Out | `Tab` on a pending block | Accepted, a splice at the cursor
Out | `Esc`, then `Esc` again | Raw words, then the block is rejected
Out, command | Show diff, then Accept on the diff, or Reject | The proposal resolves; see S20 for the queue
Out, phone | Tap the stop button on the listening sheet | The sheet closes; the pending block stays

## Data contract

What | Direction | Port or store | Shape | When
Audio of one turn | held | tab memory only | Opus at 32 kbit/s, mono | While the key is held. Never IndexedDB, never disk
Transcript | read | `new:VoiceTranscribe` through the route of `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 2.2 | text | When the turn ends
Restructured text | read | `new:VoiceRestructure` | checked text, per `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 5.5 | After the transcript, while the block is pending
Classification | read | `new:VoiceClassify` | `dictation`, `command` or `unsure` | Only for utterances that pass stage 1
Pending block | write | the change queue, as S20 reads it | a proposal: byte range, base hash, proposed bytes, author, intent | When the transcript lands; updated when the restructure returns
Command proposal | write | the change queue | the same fields; intent is the command's number, never the words | When a command is recognised
Accept | write | `ProjectFiles.splice(path, range, bytes)` with a compare-and-swap on the base hash | the proposal's bytes | On `Tab`, or Accept on the diff
Settings | read and write | the account's settings | the `voice.*` keys of `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 9 | On change, synced across devices
Entitlement | read | `limitsFor(account)` | `limits.voice.minutes`, `limits.voice.turn.seconds` | Before a turn starts

**None of this is built.** At `cb7c16f` a search of `src` for dictation, microphone and speech APIs
returned nothing (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md`, state of the repository).

## Actions

Action | Trigger | What happens | Failure behaviour | Event id
Start a turn | Hold the voice key, or tap the mic on the phone | The microphone opens and the pill or the sheet appears | Permission denied, so `new:voice-mic-denied`; no device, so `new:voice-no-mic` | `new:voice.turn.started`
End a turn | Release the key, press it again, or tap stop | The audio is sent, the raw transcript appears as a pending block | Under half a second of speech, so `new:voice-nothing-heard`; offline on the web, so `new:voice-offline` | `new:voice.turn.transcribed`
Restructure | Automatic, unless the raw setting is on | The block's text is replaced by the checked text, still pending | Too slow, so `new:voice-restructure-slow`; a check failed, so `new:voice-check-changed-words`. Either way the raw text stays | `new:voice.restructure.served`
Accept a block | `Tab`, or Accept on the phone | One splice at the block's range | Stale range, so `E027` and nothing is written | `new:voice.insertion.accepted`
Swap to raw | `Esc`, or Raw on the phone | The block shows the raw transcript, still pending | none | none
Reject a block | `Esc` a second time | The block is removed; the file is unchanged | none | `new:voice.insertion.rejected`
Give a command | Say it with a selection, or hold `Alt` or `Option` as well | A proposal on the target with the result ghosted | No target, so `new:voice-no-target`; inside a fence, table or front matter, so `new:voice-target-protected`; over 1,500 words, so `new:voice-selection-too-long` | `new:voice.command.proposed`
Show diff | The proposal's Show diff | The diff opens; Accept and Reject sit beside it at equal weight | none | none
Insert as text instead | The proposal's link | The proposal is dropped and the words land as dictation | none | none, and `71` proposes none
Change the level | The pill's chevron, or the level cards | The next turn uses the new level | none | none
Turn clean-up off | The switch in settings | Raw mode: no text-model call, commands only through the forcing key | none | none

## States

State | When | What is shown | What the person can do
first-run | The first turn on this account | A one-line sheet before the browser's own prompt: where audio goes and that it is not kept, `new:voice-first-use` | Continue, or cancel
empty | No text to act on and a command is said | `new:voice-no-target` | Select text, or speak dictation
loading | The turn ended and the transcript is on its way | The pill says it is working; nothing is inserted yet | Wait, or keep typing
partial | The transcript landed and the restructure has not | The raw words as the pending block, then replaced in place | Accept the raw words, or wait
offline | No connection, web only | `new:voice-offline`; audio is dropped, never queued | Type, or use the desktop app
unauthorised | The viewer's role may not write | The mic and the key are not offered | Read only
over-cap | The voice bucket is empty | `new:voice-cap-reached`, with the upgrade path on Free | Type, or wait for the refill
AI-unavailable | Every speech provider refused | `new:voice-busy`; nothing is kept to retry | Try again later
conflict | The document changed under a pending block | Accept is refused with `E027`; the block stays for a second look | Reject it, or speak again
error | The microphone stopped mid-turn | `new:voice-mic-lost`; what was heard so far lands as a pending block | Accept what was heard
degraded | Restructuring failed its checks or timed out | The raw words stay, with `new:voice-check-changed-words` or `new:voice-restructure-slow` | Accept the raw words
unsure | The classifier could not tell | The words land as dictation, with a chip offering the command (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 7.3). Not drawn | Tap the chip or press `Tab`, or ignore it

## Keyboard and focus

key | does
`Cmd/Ctrl + .`, held | Dictate while held
`Cmd/Ctrl + .`, pressed | Start and stop, in toggle mode
`Alt` or `Option` with the voice key | Force a command
`Tab` on a pending block | Accept it
`Esc` on a pending block | Swap to the raw words; a second `Esc` rejects
`Tab` on the unsure chip | Run the command

**Focus stays in the document** while the pill is up. The pill takes no focus, so the person can keep
typing; a keystroke inside a pending block accepts it first (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 8.2).

**`Tab` conflicts, and the pending block wins.** Outside a block, `Tab` indents a list item or moves
focus. Inside or right after a pending block it accepts. Recorded for `15-INTERACTION-AND-KEYBOARD.md`.

## Responsive

- **The mic takes the middle of the bottom bar**, and Outline steps out of the bar on voice screens.
  The outline stays in the right drawer. `INFERENCE:` this file's choice; `71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section
  10.2 says only that the mic is in the bottom bar.
- **Toggle mode is the only mode.** Tap to start, tap to stop.
- **The listening sheet carries a Command chip**, because a phone has no modifier key.
- **The pending block shows Accept and Raw buttons** instead of `Tab` and `Esc`.
- **The proposal card stacks** its actions under its line of text.

## Desktop differences

- **Speech can stay on the machine.** With the local engine on, the transcript never leaves the
  computer, and the screen says whether restructuring does (`71-VOICE-SPEC.md` section 10.3).
- **Offline dictation works**; restructuring falls back to raw when it needs the network.
- **Until the model is downloaded**, the desktop uses the cloud chain and says so.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never write a cleaned-up word without Accept.** The pending block is the rule.
- **Never accept on a timer.**
- **Never listen when the key is not held** or toggle mode is off. No wake word.
- **Never act on an unsure classification.** It is dictation with a chip.
- **Never let a command act outside the open document**: no send, share, publish, push, rename or delete.
- **Never keep audio**, to retry or for anything else.
- **Never show the spoken command words in the document's history.**
- **Never offer another language** in v1.
