## Purpose

What I have used this month, what I am allowed, and what changes if I pay.

## Entry and exit

How a person arrives | Route or control
Settings, the tenth nav entry | `/settings/plan`. It is a section of S28, not a separate page
A meter in the right rail, clicked | The same route, scrolled to the meters
S33 over the cap, the Move to Pro control | The same route, with the tripped limit marked
The phone bottom bar, More, then Plan | The same route
An email about a failed payment | The same route, with the payment state at the top

How they leave | What happens
Upgrade to Pro | Leaves to Razorpay's own checkout, returns to this route
A top-up | The same, and the meter moves when the webhook lands
Close the Settings tab | Back to the last document
Cancel | Stays here. The plan runs to the end of the paid period, and the page says which date

**Card details are never typed into our page.** `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 23 records
Cloudflare's self-serve clause forbidding card collection on a free-plan property, so checkout stays
on Razorpay's own surface.

**The four meters, exactly as drawn.**

order | meter | entitlement id | free allowance
1 | AI edits | `limits.ai.edits` | 10 a month
2 | Blueprints | `limits.ai.blueprints` | 1 a month, Low only
3 | Documents in the cloud | `limits.docs.cloud` | 50
4 | Published pages | `limits.pages.published` | 5

**Two meters count down and two count up.** Edits and blueprints show what is left of a monthly
allowance. Documents and pages show a standing total against a ceiling. The generator draws them
differently on purpose, and the copy must match: left for the first two, of for the second two.

**The prices on the card.** Free at ₹0. Pro at ₹299 a month or ₹2,499 a year, both GST inclusive,
`docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 13. Top-ups are 50 edits for ₹99 and 3 blueprints for ₹149,
`docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 13.

**None of these numbers is written in this screen's code.** Every one is read from S35 at run time,
through `limitsFor(account)`. `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 30 names that as the single
read path and says nothing else in the product reads a cap.

## Data contract

what | direction | port or store | shape | when
Resolved limits | read | `LimitsReader.forAccount()` | the full entitlement set, plan row plus any exception | On load
Usage counts | read | `UsageLedgerReader.currentPeriod()` | a count per entitlement id, and the period end | On load, and after any action that spends
Plan and price rows | read | `PlanCatalogueReader.all()` | plan name, monthly and annual price, what is included | On load. Written only by S35
Payment state | read | `SubscriptionReader.get()` | active, past due, cancelling, or none, with the effective date | On load, and on return from checkout
Checkout intent | write | `SubscriptionWriter.startCheckout(plan)` | plan id, returns a hosted URL | On clicking Upgrade or a top-up
Cancellation | write | `SubscriptionWriter.cancelAtPeriodEnd()` | nothing | On confirming cancel

**The ledger is the counter.** `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 18 defines a ledger entry as a row
holding account, kind, delta, model and cost, kept 180 days and then aggregated. This screen reads
it. S38 reads the same rows for one account. There is no second count.

## Actions

action | trigger | what happens | failure behaviour | event id
Open the page | Arrive on the route | Meters and plans render from the two reads | `E651` when the ledger cannot be read. The plan cards still render and the meters say the count is unavailable | `plan.viewed`
Upgrade to Pro | Click the Pro control | A checkout is started and the person leaves to Razorpay | `E754` names why a checkout could not be started. No charge, no plan change | `plan.upgrade.started`
Return from checkout | Come back to the route | The page shows the pending state until the webhook confirms | `E755` when the webhook has not landed inside the wait. The page says the payment is being confirmed rather than claiming failure | `plan.upgrade.completed`
Buy a top-up | Click a top-up | Same route as an upgrade, for a one-off amount | `E754` | `plan.topup.started`
Cancel | Click cancel, then confirm | The plan stays active to the end of the paid period, and the date is shown | `E754`. Nothing changes and the person is told to try again | `plan.cancelled`
See who is over | n/a on this screen | Not offered. That is S38, and only a founder sees it | n/a | n/a

**Two payment constraints that shape this screen, and neither is ours.** `[L]` Razorpay mandates
are capped at ₹15,000 by RBI circular RBI/2022-23/73 of 16 June 2022, and an Indian card gets one
attempt, F074, `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 5. One attempt means a failed payment must be re-offered
deliberately rather than retried quietly, because a quiet retry has nowhere to go.

## Keyboard and focus

- Focus lands on the page heading, so the plan and the reset date are read first.
- The meters are a list, not a control group. They are readable and not focusable.
- Tab order: the Free card's state, the Pro card's control, each top-up, then cancel.
- Every meter carries a text equivalent of its bar, because a bar alone says nothing aloud.
- `[O]` No conflict with `15-INTERACTION-AND-KEYBOARD.md`, read on 18 September. This screen claims
  no chord, section 2 assigns none to it, and section 6.1 has no S29 row to disagree with the focus
  rule above.

## Responsive

- The four meters stack two by two, then one by one at the narrowest width.
- The two plan cards stack, Free first, so the current plan is seen before the offer.
- The Team and Enterprise strip becomes one column. The generator does exactly this.
- Nothing is removed at phone width. Checkout works on the phone, and on the phone it is the more
  likely place for UPI.

## Desktop differences

- **The desktop app has no document cap**, `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 5. So the cloud documents
  meter is joined by one line saying documents on disk are not counted, rather than by a meter that
  can never fill.
- Checkout opens in the system browser, not in a Tauri window, because a payment page inside an
  embedded view is both hostile and hard to trust.
- **The Tauri build cannot open checkout in the system browser today**, checked on 18 September at
  `e0f6f89`. `[O]` `src-tauri/capabilities/default.json` grants `shell:default`, which is `allow-open`
  for `http(s)`, `mailto` and `tel` in the installed `tauri-plugin-shell-2.3.5` `permissions/default.toml`.
  But the capability has no `remote` key, and `tauri-utils-2.9.2` `src/acl/capability.rs` says a
  capability without one applies only to local URLs. The window loads `https://md.sgnk.ai`
  (`src-tauri/tauri.conf.json:19`), and `package.json` has no `@tauri-apps/plugin-shell`. Building
  this line needs both. `UNVERIFIED:` the behaviour on a running build. needs: a desktop build that
  clicks Upgrade.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never state a cap from its own source.** One read path, `limitsFor(account)`, and this screen is
  a reader. `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 30.
- **Never take a card number.** Checkout is hosted. Section 23 gives the clause that makes this a
  contractual matter and not only a preference.
- **Never retry a failed Indian card automatically.** One attempt, F074. A quiet retry cannot
  succeed and can confuse a person about whether they have paid.
- **Never remove or hide a document because a plan lapsed.** The downgrade rule is that everything
  stays readable and exportable, `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 5.
- **Never show Team or Enterprise as buyable.** They are named from day one and are not for sale.
- **Never invent a reset date.** It comes from the ledger's period, not from the client's clock.
- **Never show a meter without its number.** A bar on its own is unreadable aloud and imprecise on
  screen.
