## Purpose

Answer the deeper question set for an idea, with the evidence behind each option in view, on exactly
the screen Low uses.

## Entry and exit

Direction | How | Route or control
In | The depth selector on S12 set to Medium or High, then the send arrow | `C105`, then `/ideas/<ideaId>/questions`
In | S13 at Low, raising the depth mid-run; answers already given are kept | `C105`
In | Reopening an idea whose state is part-answered, from the ideas rail | `C104`
In | A High research pass finishing in the background, from its notification | `K.s14.researchdone`
Out | Every question answered or skipped, which writes the blueprint | S15, route `/ideas/<ideaId>/blueprint`
Out | Skip all remaining, confirmed in the modal, which also writes the blueprint | `C109`, then S15
Out | Leaving the tab; the run is saved and resumable at the page reached | S12
Out | Over the blueprint cap, or Pro not held | S33

**The route is the same route Low uses.** There is no `/ideas/<ideaId>/deep`. Depth is a field on the
idea record, not a second surface. That is the founders' instruction of 18 September, recorded at
`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:104`.

## Data contract

what | direction | port or store | shape | when
Idea record | read | `IdeaStore.get(ideaId)` | `{ id, title, text, depth, templateId, state, page, rewritesUsed }` | On arrival, and after every answer
Question set | read | `IdeaStore.questions(ideaId)` | An ordered list of `{ index, text, branching, options, recommendation }` | On arrival
Decision card body | read | `BlueprintPlanner.card(ideaId, index)` | `{ standing, forces, options, evidence, recommendation }` | When a card is first shown
Evidence rows, Medium | read | `TemplateStore.sources(templateId)` | `{ claim, source, lastChecked }` | With the card. Never a live fetch
Evidence rows, High | read | `ResearchStore.findings(ideaId)` | `{ claim, url, openedAt, quote }` | With the card, from the research pass
Answer | write | `IdeaStore.answer(ideaId, index, choice)` | `{ index, choice, takenFromRecommendation, open }` | On each answer
Rewrite of later pages | write | `BlueprintPlanner.rewrite(ideaId, fromIndex)` | A replacement question set for the unanswered tail | Only when the answered question carried the branching flag
Decision record | write | `ProjectFiles.splice('DECISIONS.md', range, bytes)` | Markdown, one heading per decision, an open marker for Not sure | On each answer, spliced, never rewritten whole
Entitlement | read | `limitsFor(account)` | `features.depth.medium`, `features.depth.high`, `limits.blueprint.rewrites`, `limits.ai.blueprints` | Before the depth is accepted and before each rewrite
Ledger | write | `UsageLedger.record` | `{ account, kind, delta, model, cost }` | On each model call

**Every port above is specified, not built.** At `0af3c90` there is no `src/modules/ideas/`. The
nearest shipped thing is `LlmClient` in `src/modules/ai/application/ports.ts:5`, which is a model
client and not a planner.

**`DECISIONS.md` is written by splice.** The engine locates a byte range and replaces those bytes. A
whole-file rewrite of a decision record is a defect, not a shortcut.

## Actions

action | trigger | what happens | failure behaviour | event id
Open the depth menu | Click the depth pill, or `D` | The three depths appear with what each asks and what it costs | none | `idea.depth.opened`
Raise the depth | Choose Medium or High | Answers already given are kept, the tail is regenerated at the new depth, one rewrite is charged | Pro not held, so `E650` offers the plan page | `idea.depth.changed`
Choose an option | Click an option button, or the number key beside it | The answer is recorded, the card advances, the decision record is spliced | Splice range ambiguous, so `E026` refuses and keeps the card open | `idea.question.answered`
Take the recommendation | The quiet link on the answer row | Same as choosing, with `takenFromRecommendation` set | as above | `idea.question.recommended`
Not sure | The quiet link at the foot of the card | The question is recorded as open, the recommendation applies for now, the record says open rather than decided | as above | `idea.question.left_open`
Skip | Skip on the page navigation | The question is left unanswered and the recommendation applies | none | `idea.question.skipped`
Next | Next, or Enter | The next card is shown. A rewrite fires only if the answer carried the branching flag | Rewrite cap reached, so `E077` falls back to the standard set | `idea.page.advanced`
Rewrite fires | A branching answer | The affected card blurs, names the answer that caused it, and is replaced | Model unavailable, so `E086` keeps the planned questions and says so | `idea.questions.rewritten`
Skip all remaining | Skip all remaining, from page two | The modal opens and states the count and the consequence | none | `idea.skipall.opened`
Confirm skip all | The confirm button in the modal | Every remaining question takes its recommendation, the blueprint is written | Over the blueprint cap, so `E072` sends to S33 | `idea.skipall.confirmed`
Start a High research pass | Choosing High | A background job starts, the screen stays usable, the finish notifies | Job fails, so `E088` offers Medium evidence and does not charge | `idea.research.started`
Leave | Closing the tab, or the browser | The run is saved at the page reached | none | `idea.run.suspended`

**`E086` is the graceful-degradation path, not an apology.** The founders' answer of 18 September
inverts the toggle: dynamic questioning is the default, and the standard question set is what the
product falls back to when the model layer is degraded or the person is over their cap. That is at
`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:359`.

## States

state | when | what is shown | what the person can do
first-run | The first Medium or High run on this account | One line naming what the deeper depths add. Shown once | Dismiss, or carry on.
empty | The idea has no text | The screen is not reachable; S12 holds the input | Go back and describe the idea.
loading | The question set is being generated, once, from the idea | The progress line and a skeleton card | Wait, or leave and resume.
partial | Some pages answered, the tail still planned | The answered count in the progress line | Carry on, skip, or skip all.
offline | No connection | Answers already given stay, the card is read-only, `E087` names the reason | Read, leave, resume when back.
unauthorised | Signed out, or the idea belongs to another account | Sign-in for the first, `E066` for the second | Sign in, or go back.
over-cap | Blueprint credits spent, or rewrites past `limits.blueprint.rewrites` | `E072` for credits, `E077` for rewrites | Top up, wait for the reset, or go to Pro.
AI-unavailable | Every provider in the chain refused or timed out | `E086`, the answers untouched, nothing charged | Use the standard question set, retry, or use the local model on the desktop.
conflict | The decision record changed on disk under an open run | S31, both versions kept, nothing merged | Keep one, keep both, or let AI propose a merge into the queue.
error | The planner returned a set that fails its own schema | `E564`, the run held at the last good page | Retry, or drop to the standard set.
degraded | High research finished with fewer sources than asked | The card shows what was opened and says how many | Answer on what is there, or rerun the pass.

## Keyboard and focus

key | does
Tab, Shift Tab | Moves through options, then the answer row, then the page navigation
1 to 9 | Chooses the option with that number
Enter | Next, when an answer is set
R | Takes the recommendation
O | Marks the question open, which is Not sure
D | Opens the depth menu
Escape | Closes the depth menu, then the skip-all modal

**Focus on arrival is the first option of the current card**, so a person answering by keyboard never
touches the mouse. **Focus on a rewrite moves to the replaced card**, and the reason line is announced
in a live region, because a blur that nobody is told about reads as a fault.

**Checked 18 September, no collision `[O]`.** `grep -nE '^\`[A-Za-z0-9/]\`' docs/pack/15-INTERACTION-AND-KEYBOARD.md`
finds one bare key, `/`, and it lives inside the editor. The window map in its section 2 uses only
modified chords. The editor's bare `Enter` and `Tab` apply inside CodeMirror, which this screen does
not show.

- **One condition keeps it true.** `1` to `9`, `R`, `O` and `D` act only while focus is outside a text
  field, so typing into a free-text option never answers a question.
- **Rule 2 of file 15 holds.** Nothing here is destructive: an answer can be changed until the
  blueprint is written.
- **Escape fits the stack** in file 15 section 5 at layer 6, one layer per press.
- **File 15 does not list these keys yet**, and its section 13 says it should. Recorded in the log.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never fork the route.** Medium and High are a field on the idea, never a second surface to learn.
- **Never show a confidence percentage** on a recommendation. Show the alternatives instead.
- **Never write Not sure as the founder's decision.** The record says open.
- **Never cite evidence it did not read.** Medium may cite the person's documents and the template,
  with the date the template last checked. High may cite only pages it opened, with the date.
- **Never rewrite the decision record whole.** Splice the range, or refuse.
- **Never blur a card that did not change.** The processing state is rare so that it means something.
- **Never charge for a page turn** that fired no model call.
- **Never let a failed research pass charge a credit.**
- **Never hide the cost** of raising the depth before the depth is raised.
