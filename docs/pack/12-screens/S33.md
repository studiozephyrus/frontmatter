## Purpose

I have hit a limit, everything I already have still works, and here is exactly how to get going
again.

## Entry and exit

**The shape is three sentences: what happened, what still works, what to do.** That is the plan's
own order in section 5, and the screen keeps it.

how a person arrives | which cap tripped
Creating the 51st cloud document | `limits.docs.cloud`
Publishing the 6th page | `limits.pages.published`
Asking for the 11th AI edit in a month | `limits.ai.edits`
Starting a second blueprint in a month | `limits.ai.blueprints`
Uploading past 1 GB | `limits.uploads.total`. A single file over the per-file size is `E038`, not this screen, `D75`
Inviting a second live collaborator | `limits.collab.live`
Connecting a second repository, or the 21st push | `limits.github.repos`, `limits.github.pushes`
A Pro plan lapsing back to Free | Whichever cap the account is now above
A founder lowering a limit on S35 | The same, for every account the change moves over the line
A time-boxed exception from S38 expiring | The same, for that one account

how they leave | what happens
Delete or export something | Back to the workspace, under the cap
Use the desktop app | The desktop has no document cap and is named on the screen
Move to Pro | Razorpay's hosted checkout, then back
Wait for the reset | Nothing to do. Monthly allowances reset on a stated date, shown on S29
Dismiss | Back to the workspace. Everything opens, edits and exports

**Only the third row changes per cap.** The first two are true of every cap in the table above, and
they are the reason this screen is not a wall.

## Data contract

what | direction | port or store | shape | when
Resolved limits | read | `LimitsReader.forAccount()` | the full entitlement set, plan row plus any exception | Before every create, publish, upload, invite and model call
Current counts | read | `UsageLedgerReader.currentPeriod()` | a count per entitlement id | The same moment
Which cap tripped | read | the refusal itself | the entitlement id and both numbers | On the refusal
Reset date | read | `UsageLedgerReader.periodEnd()` | a date | On render, for the monthly caps only
Exception | read | `LimitsReader.forAccount()` | any exception and its expiry | On render, so a granted exception is visible rather than mysterious

**One read path, and this screen is downstream of it.** Section 30 names a single function,
`limitsFor(account)`, and says nothing else in the product reads a cap. This screen reads the
refusal that function produced. It does not do its own arithmetic, because a second calculation is
a second source of truth.

**No write of any kind.** This screen deletes nothing, prunes nothing, archives nothing and queues
nothing. Its only outward action is starting a checkout, which S29 owns.

## Desktop differences

- **The desktop app has no document cap**, and section 5 says it is named on this screen. On the
  desktop itself, the row that names it is replaced by a line saying documents on disk are not
  counted.
- The caps that do apply on the desktop are the ones that spend our money or our pools: AI edits,
  blueprints, published pages, live collaborators, repositories and pushes.
- So this screen is reachable on the desktop, but never for the document count.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never delete anything.** Not the oldest document, not an unused upload, not a draft, not a
  published page, and not after a grace period. Section 13's downgrade row and section 30 both say
  everything stays readable and exportable.
- **Never hide anything.** Read-only is not the same as invisible, and a document that cannot be
  found has effectively been taken.
- **Never stop an export.** The export is the way out, and a cap that blocks it makes the product
  a trap.
- **Never unpublish a page for being over the cap.** A published page has readers who are not the
  account holder.
- **Never block a person from opening what they already have.** The refusal is on creation only.
- **Never show a cap the account is not actually over.** The numbers are read, not assumed.
- **Never blame the person when the limit moved.** A lowered limit gets its own line, because it is
  our change and not their usage.
- **Never show this screen and S32 together.** If the chain is also down and the person is over the
  cap, this screen wins, because the cap refused the call before any provider was asked. Corrected on
  18 September to agree with `27-MODEL-ROUTING-SPEC.md` section 7.1 rung 7.
- **Never make it undismissable.** The workspace behind is dimmed, not disabled.
