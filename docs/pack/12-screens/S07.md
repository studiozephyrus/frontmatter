# S07. AI edit

> Seven verbs on a selection, and a suggestion that appears where the words are. The founders
> reviewed it on 18 September and said it is fine as drawn, so this file specifies what is there.

> **Ids invented here.** `14-COMPONENT-INVENTORY.md`, `16-COPY-DECK.md`,
> `17-ERROR-AND-REFUSAL-CATALOGUE.md`, `19-ACCEPTANCE-CRITERIA.md`,
> `55-MEASUREMENT-AND-EVENTS.md` and `56-OPEN-DECISIONS.md` are being written in parallel and did
> not exist when this file was written. Ids follow `65-CONVENTIONS.md` section 3 on a stated
> scheme derived from the screen number: components `C070` to `C079`, errors `E070` to `E079`,
> acceptance `A070` to `A079`. A component owned by another screen is cited by that screen's id.
> Copy ids are namespaced `K.s07.*`, so they cannot collide.

## Purpose

Change a chosen piece of text with one verb, see the change where the text is, and keep or discard
it with two controls of equal weight.

## Entry and exit

Direction | Route or control | Goes to | Notes
Arrive | select text, then the AI control | `C070` opens beside the selection | no route change
Arrive, keyboard | the AI shortcut with a selection | the same | see Keyboard and focus
Arrive, phone | long-press to select, then AI | `C078`, a bottom sheet | the phone form of the menu
Leave, a verb | tap a verb in `C070` | one call, then the suggestion in place | the menu closes on choice
Leave, Escape | Escape | the menu closes, nothing is called | the selection survives
Leave, Accept | Accept in `C076` | the splice lands, the suggestion clears | one version, one ledger entry
Leave, Reject | Reject in `C076` | the suggestion clears, the document is untouched | the credit is still spent, because the call ran
Leave, no selection | the AI control with nothing selected | S06 instead | an empty selection is a different job

**The entry condition is a selection.** Without one this is S06, not this screen, and the product
never has to guess which of the two a person meant.

### The seven verbs

The order is fixed. Refine is first because it is the one most people want, and it is highlighted
on open without being run.

Position | Verb | What it does | Built today
1 | Refine selection | polish prose, fix grammar | `src/app/api/ai/refine/route.ts` and `src/modules/ai/application/refine-text.ts`
2 | Expand | add detail and depth | not built
3 | Shorten | more concise, same meaning | not built
4 | Change tone | professional or casual | not built
5 | Translate | keep the markdown intact | not built
6 | Summarise into a callout | adds a summary block | `src/app/api/ai/summarize/route.ts` and `application/summarize.ts`
7 | Suggest links | wiki links to your notes | `src/app/api/ai/suggest-links/route.ts` and `application/suggest-links.ts`

- **Three of the seven exist in the shipped code.** The other four are `specified, not built`.
- Two further AI routes exist that are not verbs on this menu: `api/ai/complete` for ghost text, and
  `api/ai/link-doctor`. Neither belongs here.
- **Translate keeps the markdown intact**, which means the marks, the link targets and the fence
  contents are preserved and only the prose changes.
- **Summarise into a callout adds a block**, so its splice is an insertion beside the selection
  rather than a replacement of it. It is the one verb that does not replace.

### The menu foot names the provider

`docs/mvp0/SCREENS.md:106` requires the menu to say which provider the month runs on. It is one
line, and it carries three facts.

Fact | Why it is there
One credit each | so the cost is known before the click, not after
How many are left | so a person can decide whether to spend one here
Which provider this month | the plan's free chain moves between providers, and a person is entitled to know whose machine read their words

## Data contract

What | Direction | Port or store | Shape | When
The selection | in | the editor's own state | a byte range plus its text | on the AI control
Credit balance | in | `LedgerReader.balance()` | used against the cap | when the menu opens
Provider for the month | in | `ModelRouter.currentProvider("edit")` | a provider name | when the menu opens
The request | out | `LlmClient.generate({prompt, system})` | a string | on a verb
The proposal | in | the same call | text, held in memory, never applied | on return
Accept | out | `DocumentWriter.splice(range, bytes)` | the original range and the new bytes | on Accept only
Ledger entry | out | `LedgerWriter.append()` | account, kind, delta, model, cost | when the call returns
Version record | out | `VersionWriter.append()` | a version marked as an AI edit accepted by this person | on Accept

- `LlmClient` at `src/modules/ai/application/ports.ts` is the real port and has one member,
  `generate`. Everything else above is `specified, not built`.
- **The splice uses the range captured when the verb was chosen.** If the document moved under it,
  the splice is refused rather than applied at the new offsets.
- **The version record marks the edit as AI.** The plan keeps attribution in the version record, and
  S21 shows it there.

## Actions

Action | Trigger | What happens | Failure behaviour | Event id
Open the menu | the AI control with a selection | `C070` beside the selection | empty selection `E070`, and S06 opens instead | `ai.menu.opened`
Choose a verb | tap in `C070` | one call; the menu closes; progress shows on the selection | ambiguous range `E071`; chain down `E072`; over the cap `E073`; selection too large `E076`; role cannot edit `E077`; verb unavailable on this provider `E078`; unresolved conflict `E079` | `ai.request.sent`
Proposal arrives | the call returns | `C074` and `C075` in place, `C076` beneath | the model returned the input unchanged `E074` | `ai.result.shown`
Accept | Accept in `C076` | splices, writes a version marked as an AI edit | the document moved under the range `E075`, and nothing is written | `ai.result.accepted`
Reject | Reject in `C076` | clears the proposal, the document is untouched | none | `ai.result.rejected`
Escape | Escape | closes the menu, or clears a live proposal | none | `ai.menu.dismissed`
Undo after Accept | the editor's undo | reverses the splice as one step | none | `doc.edit.undone`

**Returning the input unchanged is a correct outcome**, not a failure to apologise for. `E074` says
the model had nothing to change and no credit is charged twice for asking again.

## States

State | When | What is shown | What the person can do
first-run | the first AI edit on this account | the menu, plus one line on what a credit buys | choose a verb, or close
empty | no selection | the menu never opens; S06 opens instead | write a prompt there
loading | the call is in flight | the selection marked as working, the menu closed | wait, or press Escape to abandon the result
partial | the proposal is streaming | `C075` filling in, with Accept disabled until it completes | wait, or cancel
offline | no network | the menu opens and every verb is disabled with one reason | close; on the desktop a local model runs the verb
unauthorised | the role is Commenter or Viewer | the AI control is absent, not disabled-and-mysterious | comment instead
over-cap | no credits left | the menu opens, verbs disabled, the foot names the reset date | top up, wait, or use the desktop
AI-unavailable | every provider refused or timed out | `E072`: the selection is untouched, nothing was charged, and the chain's state is listed | retry, or open S32
conflict | the document has an unresolved conflict | the menu refuses to open | resolve on S31 first
error | the splice was refused | `C079` in place of `C076`, naming the range and why | change the selection and ask again
degraded | the chain is on a slower fallback | the menu works, and the foot names the provider | choose a verb

## Keyboard and focus

- With a selection, the AI shortcut opens the menu and focus lands on the first verb.
- Up and down move through the verbs. Enter chooses one. Escape closes.
- On a proposal, focus moves to Accept, with Reject one Tab away.
- Command Z after Accept undoes the splice as a single step, not character by character.
- The selection is never lost while the menu is open, and it is restored if the menu is dismissed.
- The full map is `15-INTERACTION-AND-KEYBOARD.md`; this screen registers no shortcut of its own
  beyond the shared AI one.

## Responsive

- The menu becomes a bottom sheet, titled with the verb group, listing the same seven in the same
  order.
- The selection stays highlighted behind the sheet, so the target is still visible.
- The decision pair sits directly under the proposed span, not in a floating bar, because a floating
  bar at thumb height covers the text it is about.
- The AI item in the bottom bar is lit while the sheet is open.
- Long-press selects, as the platform does; the product adds no selection gesture of its own.

## Desktop differences

- With the network chain down, a local model runs the verb and no credit is charged. The foot says
  so, and says nothing left the machine.
- Translate on a local model is offered only when the installed model supports it, and the verb is
  disabled with that reason rather than failing after a call.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never apply a verb's result without a click.** The plan names AI edits you cannot switch off as
  a switching driver at 21 mentions, so this is a competitive position, not a preference.
- **Never guess a byte range.** An ambiguous range is refused and shown in `C079`, with the document
  untouched.
- **Never re-resolve the range at Accept time.** The range captured at the verb is the range that
  splices, or nothing splices.
- **Never make Reject quieter than Accept.**
- **Never pre-run the highlighted verb** just because it is highlighted.
- **Never hide which provider read the text.**
- **Never charge twice for one call.**
- **Never write an AI edit into history without marking it as one.**
- **Never open this menu with an empty selection.** That case belongs to S06.
- **Never let Translate rewrite a link target, a fence body or a front matter key.**
- **Never use an emoji on a verb row.**
