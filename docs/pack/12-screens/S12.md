# S12. Ideas

> Rebuilt on 18 September. The old screen put a four-step breadcrumb, three large depth cards and
> two columns in front of a person before they had typed anything. This one is a centred column
> with one input.

> **Ids invented here.** `14-COMPONENT-INVENTORY.md`, `16-COPY-DECK.md`,
> `17-ERROR-AND-REFUSAL-CATALOGUE.md`, `19-ACCEPTANCE-CRITERIA.md`,
> `55-MEASUREMENT-AND-EVENTS.md` and `56-OPEN-DECISIONS.md` are being written in parallel and did
> not exist when this file was written. Ids follow `65-CONVENTIONS.md` section 3 on a stated
> scheme derived from the screen number: components `C120` to `C129`, errors `E120` to `E129`,
> acceptance `A120` to `A129`. `C120` and `C121` are cited by S14 with the meanings given below.
> Copy ids are namespaced `K.s12.*`, so they cannot collide.

> **Error ids are provisional, and they clash.** `17-ERROR-AND-REFUSAL-CATALOGUE.md` now exists and
> is the home for every error id. It allocates by **category**, not by screen: engine `E001` to
> `E025`, validation `E030` to `E041`, permission `E050` to `E060`, quota `E070` to `E079`, model
> `E080` to `E087`, provider `E090` to `E097`, network `E100` to `E104`, conflict `E110` to `E117`.
> The `E` ids below were allocated from this screen's own block before that register existed, so
> many of them name a different error there. **Read them as descriptions, not as keys**, and
> reconcile each one into its category block. Every row states what the error is, so the mapping is
> mechanical. Component, acceptance and copy ids are unaffected.

## Purpose

Take an idea in a person's own words, with whatever they want to attach to it, and choose how deep
to go without making that choice feel like picking a product.

## The founders' instructions

All `[Z]`, from `docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:101` to `:104`.

Instruction | How it lands
Rethink the whole interface, phone included; it is cognitive-load-heavy today | one centred column, one input, everything else folded into it
The reference points are Gemini, Claude chat and ChatGPT chat | `C122` is a chat composer, not a form
Low, Medium and High should read like **Claude's model selector**, not like three routes | `C121` is a small pill in the composer's bar row, beside the attachment controls
Medium and High keep the same interface as Low; do not fork the route | S13 and S14 share this composer and this layout

**The depth is a property of the run, not a product to choose between.** That is the whole reason
it moved from three large cards into a pill the size of an attachment button.

### The idea rail, and what a state says

Each row is an idea and the second line is its state. The three states in the drawn example are the
three that exist.

State | What it means | Where it goes
Draft | typed, never sent | back into `C124`
A count answered, such as nine of twelve | part way through the questions | S13 or S14, at that question
A blueprint version | finished, with a file count | S15

- **Answers are never lost when the depth changes.** The plan says start at Low and go deeper later
  without losing answers, and the rail is where a person comes back to do that.
- The rail foot carries the blueprint credits left for the month, because that is the number that
  decides whether the arrow can be pressed.

### The depth selector

Depth | Plan | Questions | What each answer carries
Low | Free | 10 to 15 | a recommendation and one line of reason
Medium | Pro | 20 to 30 | where it stands, what forces the choice, options with gains and costs, evidence from the person's own documents and the template's sources
High | Pro, three blueprint credits | 20 to 30 | Medium, plus a research pass before the questions, with sources opened and dated

- The menu shows each depth with its plan badge and one line, in the shape of a model picker.
- **Choosing a depth does not change the layout.** S14 is drawn with the same progress line, the
  same selector pill and the same navigation as S13.
- **`[X]` The plan's section 9 lists seven industry templates**: local service business, SaaS,
  marketplace, internal tool, mobile app, content site and agency. `C127` draws four of them plus
  One for my industry. Three are not reachable from this screen as drawn. See D34a.

### Attachments

Control | What it takes | What the blueprint does with it
A drawing or a screenshot | an image, or an Excalidraw file | the frontend spec describes the screens and names them
A document | any document in the workspace | context for the questions
A repository | a connected repository | context, and a target for the kit

The attachment chip states what the attachment will be used for, in the same words the kit will
use. A person who attaches a drawing is told the blueprint will describe those screens.

## Data contract

What | Direction | Port or store | Shape | When
Idea list | in | `IdeaReader.list()` | id, name, state, blueprint version | on mount
Blueprint credits | in | `LedgerReader.balance("blueprint")` | used against the cap | on mount
Templates | in | `TemplateReader.listIndustry()` | seven descriptors | on mount
Draft idea text | out | the device draft store | a string | on every keystroke, debounced
Attachment | out | `AttachmentWriter.add()` | a file or a reference | on attach
Depth | out | the idea record | one of three | on change
Create the idea | out | `IdeaWriter.create()` | text, depth, attachments | on send
Generate the question set | out | `LlmClient.generate()` | the whole set, in one call | on send, and see S13

- **Nothing is sent to a model until the arrow is pressed.** `C128` says so on the screen, and it is
  the reason a person can type freely here.
- **The question set is generated in one call**, which is the 18 September decision, and it happens
  on this screen's send rather than on S13's arrival. S13 renders with the set already planned.
- `IdeaReader`, `IdeaWriter`, `TemplateReader` and `AttachmentWriter` are `specified, not built`.

## States

State | When | What is shown | What the person can do
first-run | no ideas yet | S34 instead of this screen | read what a blueprint is, then start
empty | the composer has no text | the column, with the send arrow disabled | type, or pick a template
loading | the question set is being generated | the column, with progress on the arrow | wait; this is the one wait on this screen
partial | the idea list is read, the credit count is not | the rail without its foot number | type; the number lands before the arrow is needed
offline | no network | the composer works and the draft is kept | type; the arrow is disabled with the reason
unauthorised | the session expired | routes to S01, draft kept locally | sign in; nothing is lost
over-cap | no blueprint credit left | the composer works, the arrow is disabled, the foot names the reset date | wait, top up, or use the desktop
AI-unavailable | the chain is down | the arrow is disabled and says the document is untouched and nothing was charged | retry, or see S32
conflict | n/a | n/a, an unsent idea cannot conflict | n/a
error | generation failed | the typed idea is kept in full, with the reason | retry; the text is never lost
degraded | the chain is on a fallback | the arrow works and the foot names the provider | send

**A failed send never loses the typed idea.** It is the only thing on this screen a person cannot
get back, so it is kept in the draft store before the call and cleared only after the set lands.

## Keyboard and focus

- On arrival focus is in the text area, because the first act is to type.
- Command Enter sends. Enter inserts a newline, because an idea is prose.
- The depth pill opens on Enter or Space and closes on Escape; up and down move between the three.
- Tab from the text area reaches the bar row as one composite stop, then the send arrow.
- The template chips are one composite stop below the composer.
- The rail is a list widget with arrow-key navigation, not one tab stop per idea.

## Responsive

- One column, full width, with the same composer. It was already one column, which is why the
  rebuild works on the phone without a second design.
- The ideas rail becomes the left drawer.
- **The depth pill stays in the bar row.** It is the founders' selector and it is small enough to
  stay.
- The template chips reduce from five to three, keeping the selected one first.
- The attachment row keeps the drawing and the document controls; the repository control moves into
  an overflow.

## Desktop differences

- **Medium and High can run on a local model**, free, with nothing leaving the machine. The
  founders' proposal at `docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:262` makes that the desktop app's
  reason to exist, and the depth menu says so on those two rows.
- A repository attachment can point at a folder on disk as well as a connected remote.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never present the three depths as three routes or three products.** They are a selector, in the
  shape of a model picker.
- **Never fork the layout by depth.** S13 and S14 are the same screen with different cards in it.
- **Never lose answers when the depth changes.**
- **Never send anything to a model before the arrow is pressed.**
- **Never lose the typed idea on a failure.**
- **Never put a breadcrumb, a step counter or a wizard chrome on this screen.** That is what the
  rebuild removed.
- **Never show a second column.**
- **Never claim an attachment will be used in a way the kit does not use it.**
- **Never charge a blueprint credit before the fifteen files are listed.** S13 lists them.
- **Never use an emoji on a chip, a control or a depth row.**
