### What must change in the record

Any operational date in the record derived from 2026-03-01 is five days late.
2. **Correct "DigiCert token +120" to "+$144.00 per 12-month subscription (OV and EV alike)."** Show the subtraction — $840.00 − $696.00 — because the wrong number came from multiplying a monthly headline that is not one-twelfth of the billed price.
