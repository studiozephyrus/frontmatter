# Eighteen decisions before the final plan

Pick one letter per question. Where an option says "write it", add a line. Ten questions change the shape of the plan and come first; eight can wait for the build's first fortnight. Approving every default makes plan v5 the final plan.

## Part one. Ten that gate the final plan

### 1. The pace, and which phases are Later

Distinct days with a code commit: 1.21 a week over 58 days, one author on every commit; twenty-four weeks of full-time appetite is 99 to 129 calendar weeks at that pace.

- A. Measured pace, plan v5 default. Phases 0, A, B, D and H ship at the measured pace with dates published every Friday; E, F and G move to Later; a contractor is engaged for D and F if the pace has not doubled by the pilot.
- B. Both founders full time. The phase table stands as written, twenty-four weeks.
- C. A contractor from day one. Phases A and B run in parallel with D, and the pilot comes forward by about eight weeks.

### 2. Pro price and routing

At the caps, Pro on Sonnet 5 costs ₹321 a month (₹367 on the fourteen-file blueprint) against ₹246 net of GST and Razorpay.

- A. Plan v5 default. ₹299 stays; edits run on Haiku 4.5 and blueprints on Sonnet 5 through the batch API; about ₹60 of margin per Pro user before fixed costs.
- B. Sonnet 5 for everything, and the price rises to ₹499 or more.
- C. Sonnet 5 for everything at ₹299, with the caps cut to 50 edits and 3 blueprints.
- D. Keep ₹299, the caps and Sonnet, and accept that Pro loses about ₹120 per active user per month.

### 3. The free chain and the sign-in promise

The sign-in page promises "We never train on your documents". OpenRouter's Nvidia-served endpoints have terms nobody opened; Cerebras is a 30-day trial.

- A. Plan v5 default. Groq, Cloudflare Workers AI, Cerebras while the trial lasts, SambaNova, and the local model on the desktop; no OpenRouter; the promise stays and links to the provider list; paid Cloudflare neurons from about 260 active free users.
- B. The same chain plus OpenRouter's Nvidia endpoints, once their provider's terms are opened and quoted with no training.
- C. Add a provider whose terms permit training, and change the line to "We do not train on your documents; some providers on the free plan may".

### 5. K1, the product in one sentence

- A. Plan v5 default. "a markdown editor for people whose documents are increasingly written with, and for, AI agents".
- B. "The best markdown note-taking app in the world". The customer becomes group three and Ideas moves behind the editor in every phase.
- C. Another sentence. Write it.

### 6. K2, which bytes we hold, from which phase

Sign-in first and cloud documents on Free make holding unavoidable from phase A; card K2 said hold as little as possible for as long as possible.

- A. Plan v5 default. Every cloud document is held from phase A, under the deletion, export and retention rules in the data model.
- B. The desktop app carries the first pilot and the web holds only shared pages. Phase F moves before A, and the free caps mean nothing until sync exists.

### 8. Live editing and the CRDT ban

Yjs is a CRDT. Revision 5 restores the exception: session state lives only while two people have the file open, every save is a splice, nothing from the session persists.

- A. Plan v5 default. Live editing stays in phase D on that rule.
- B. Cut live editing to Later. Phase D shrinks by about a week, and the three-collaborator cap becomes a sharing cap.

### 9. The free caps

The market floor is 50 documents across forty pricing pages; a blueprint alone is fifteen files.

- A. Plan v5 default. 50 documents, 5 published pages, 3 live collaborators; a downgraded account keeps every document readable and exportable and cannot create new ones until under the cap.
- B. 5, 2 and 1. The import and idea funnels block on day one, and the plan says so.
- C. Other numbers. Write them.

### 11. Desktop before or after sync, and who signs Windows

Azure Artifact Signing excludes Indian entities; Apple is 99 USD a year.

- A. Plan v5 default. Desktop after Drive sync; macOS signed on the Apple programme; Linux unsigned by choice; Windows shown as coming until a commercial certificate is priced.
- B. Desktop first. Phase F moves before E, and the Windows certificate is answered before, not after.

### 14. The twenty hand-made kits

The 13 September plan and revision 3 gated everything on twenty kits; revision 4 dropped the gate.

- A. Plan v5 default. Phase 0 returns: twenty kits by hand for twenty people outside the studio; five of twenty run the kickoff and two of ten edit the kit again before phase C starts.
- B. Ideas ships on conviction, and the kill line moves to the pilot.

### 17. Indexing of published pages

The plan has no growth channel other than search, and the code today keeps every published page out of search engines. No default in v5.

- A. Indexed by default, with an unlisted switch on every page.
- B. Never indexed. Search is not a channel, and the plan must name another.
- C. Unlisted by default, indexed only when the author switches it on for that page.

## Part two. Eight that can wait for the build's first fortnight

### 7. K3, the name

- A. Plan v5 default. "frontmatter" stays; a founder runs the register search before Razorpay goes live; the collision is carried as a risk.

### 10. Bring-your-own key

- A. Plan v5 default. A key field in Settings AI in phase B for Anthropic and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint; a person's calls run through their key when present; the operator-paid chain is the fallback.
- B. Operator-paid only. Budget the free pools for abuse and keep the per-account breaker tight.

### 12. The age floor

DPDP section 9 requires verifiable parental consent for anyone under eighteen; the Gazette puts section 9 in force on 14 May 2027.

- A. Plan v5 default. Eighteen, stated in the terms and in the sign-in page's fine print.
- B. Thirteen, with a consent path built before 14 May 2027 and confirmed by counsel.

### 13. Accounts that move to the company

The Cloudflare zone sits on a personal account by deliberate choice; the domain, Razorpay, Apple and the provider accounts are unnamed.

- A. Plan v5 default. The zone, the domain and Razorpay move to the company before the first rupee; Apple and the providers at phase F; an ownership table in the plan.
- B. Some stay personal. Write which and why; the risk row records it.

### 15. The pilot's stop and continue lines

- A. Plan v5 default. Stop if fewer than four of twenty are active in week two, fewer than two of ten kit recipients run the kickoff, or fewer than three of twenty name the problem unprompted. Continue on six of twenty, three of ten, and one person asking how to pay.
- B. Your own numbers. Write them.

### 16. Which earlier positions stand

The Zed rebuttal is owed; review state is dropped for the change queue; the extension form factor, the community and the Max tier are deferred.

- A. Plan v5 default. The rebuttal written before phase D; the review sidecar dropped; the extension not planned; community and the Max tier Later.
- B. Bring one back: the sidecar, the extension, the community or the Max tier. Name it, and the phase table absorbs it.

### 18. The fallback sign-in

The stack section names Google and GitHub as providers, and an email magic link as the fallback sign-in for the founders to decide on.

- A. The plan's candidate. An email magic link as the fallback beside Google and GitHub.
- B. Google and GitHub only, with no email sign-in.
