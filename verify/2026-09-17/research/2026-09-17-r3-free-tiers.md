## F. Recommendation for frontmatter

Numbers, each with its comparable:

1. Cloud documents: do not cap by count. Cap free cloud sync by storage at 1 GB (Craft's free figure; Nuclino and Confluence give 2 GB), with unlimited local and repo-backed documents (Obsidian, Tolaria, AFFiNE, Anytype all do this). If a count is unavoidable, 50 is the market floor (Nuclino, Evernote, UpNote). Yes, 5 is below the market norm; the nearest peer is Figma's 3 files, a heavy design tool with unlimited drafts.
2. Public shared documents: unlimited, on a frontmatter subdomain; gate the custom domain and branding removal to paid (Notion's 1 domain, GitBook's custom domain on Premium, Nuclino's custom domain on Business). A cap of 2 has no comparable.
3. Live collaborators: 3 (HackMD "3 invitees", AFFiNE "Up to 3 members per Workspace"). Gate guests and private spaces to paid (Confluence Standard, Notion Business).
4. Version history: 7 days free, 30 days on the first paid tier (Notion 7/30, Craft 7/30, AFFiNE 7/30, Nuclino none/30, Superhuman Docs 30 on Pro).
5. AI: credit-metered, not included. Every free tier that has AI puts a number on it (Kiro 50, Tana 50, Mem 25, Canva 20, Craft 15, GitBook 10/week, ChatPRD 3) and the two tools that include AI in a $10 seat still cap it (Slite 30 questions and 30 edits per month) or charge for it separately (Notion's "$7 AI add-on", per Craft's FAQ). A free allowance in the 25 to 50 per month band matches Kiro, Tana and Mem; the paid tier should carry a monthly budget with top-ups (Kiro $0.04/credit, Capacities Plus, Canva AI Pass, Tana top-ups). "Low idea mode" should be stated as that number; no page opened uses a qualitative label.
6. GitHub connection: free with a quota, unlimited on the first paid tier. That is exactly HackMD ("20 GitHub pushes per month" free, unlimited at $5) and GitBook (Git Sync free). The tools that push GitHub to $20 or more (Notion, Tana, CodeGuide) are not git-native; for a markdown editor for repositories the connection is the product, and hiding it behind a higher tier would gate the funnel itself. Reserve GitHub Enterprise Server, SSO and IP allowlisting for a top tier (Linear and GitBook precedent).
7. First paid price: the cluster is $4 to $10 per user per month (Obsidian 4, Anytype 4, HackMD 5, Confluence 5.42, Docmost 6, AFFiNE 6.75, Craft 7.99, Nuclino 8, Mem 9, Capacities 9.99, Notion, Slite, Outline, Linear, Reflect 10); AI-forward tools sit at $15 to $20 (ChatPRD, Cursor, Kiro, Tana, Claude).
