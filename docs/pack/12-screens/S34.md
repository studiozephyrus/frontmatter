## What this screen must never do

- **Never show a blank pane.** The plan's stated reason is the usability rule against empty states,
  and an idea list is empty for everyone on their first day.
- **Never spend a credit to look.** The example kit is hand-made and read-only precisely so that
  someone can read all fifteen files before deciding.
- **Never hide the Pro depths from a Free account.** They are shown, marked, and readable. Hiding
  them makes the free product feel smaller than it is and the upgrade less legible.
- **Never create an idea before the person submits.** Opening, reading, templating and changing
  depth all write nothing.
- **Never fork the route for Medium and High.** `[Z]` the same interface, because forking confuses
  people.
- **Never make this screen a tour.** The founders' standing rule is no captcha, no puzzle, no tour.
  One paragraph, three rows, one box.
- **Never claim a kit will build the thing.** The output is a brief, fifteen files, a consistency
  check and a link, and the copy stays inside that.
