## Purpose

Give one document to named people, to a link, or to the public, and say plainly what each of those
three lets somebody do.

## Three decisions this screen carries

**People are added by their frontmatter email address.** Not by a name, not by a handle, and not by a
link. That is the founders' instruction of 18 September, at
`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:136`.

**If the address is not a frontmatter account, the screen says so and offers an invite.** Both sides
get five AI credits when the invited person signs in for the first time, at
`docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 5, at line 509. **The same modal is reused for referrals**
afterwards, at `docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:139`. The offer is not an error state, and the
copy deck marks it as such on `K.s17.invite.title`.

**One live collaborator on Free. Several on Pro.** Confirmed on cost grounds on 18 September, at
`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:305`, and section 13 of the plan was changed from three to one
to match, at `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 13, at line 1106. The market says three. We differ
because a live session is a connection held open for as long as two people are in it, and that is the
one free-tier cost that scales with time rather than with calls. S19 is where the limit is met.

**Three blocks, in falling order of trust.** People are named and signed in. A link is a secret string.
A published page is public. The dialog reads top to bottom in that order and never reorders them.

## Data contract

what | direction | port or store | shape | when
Current shares | read | `ShareSnapshotPort` | The document's people, links and published state | On open
Account lookup | read | `AccountDirectory.byEmail(address)` | `{ exists: true, accountId }` or `{ exists: false }` | On a complete address, debounced
Add a person | write | `ShareWriter.set` | `{ documentId, accountId, role }` | On add
Change a role | write | `ShareWriter.set` | The same shape with the new role | On change
Remove a person | write | `ShareWriter.remove` | `{ documentId, accountId }` | On remove
Invite | write | `InviteStore.create` | `{ fromAccount, toEmail, credits, state }` | On send
Invite redemption | write | `InviteStore.redeem` | Credits to both sides, once, at the invited person's first sign-in | Not here. On their sign-in
Link | write | `ShareWriter.set` | `{ token, role, expiresAt, passwordHash }` | On any link row change
Password | write | `ShareWriter.set` | A hash only. The plain password is never stored and never logged | On set
Publish | write | `PublishStore.publish` | `{ documentId, slug, branded }` | On the publish switch
Entitlement | read | `limitsFor(account)` | `limits.collab.live`, `features.links.password`, `limits.pages.published` | Before each of those three controls acts

**`ShareWriter`, `ShareSnapshotPort`, `set-share.ts` and `remove-share.ts` exist** in
`src/modules/share/application/` at `0af3c90`. `AccountDirectory`, `InviteStore` and `PublishStore` do
not, and are **specified, not built**.

**The password is stored as a hash.** The dialog says so on the row, because a person choosing a
password for a document deserves to know what we keep.

## Actions

action | trigger | what happens | failure behaviour | event id
Type an address | The people input | After a complete address, the directory is asked whether it is an account | Lookup fails, so `E330` says the check could not run and offers the invite anyway | `share.address.checked`
Add a known person | Enter, or the add control | They appear in the people list at the chosen role | Already added, so nothing happens and the row is highlighted | `share.person.added`
Meet an unknown address | The lookup returns no account | The invite block appears, says the address is not on frontmatter, and offers the invite | none | `share.invite.offered`
Send an invite | The invite control | An invite is recorded. Both sides get five AI credits when that person signs in for the first time, and not before | Invite already open to that address, so `E331` says so | `share.invite.sent`
Open the referral | The referral entry point elsewhere in the product | The same invite block, same terms | none | `share.referral.opened`
Change a role | The role selector on a row | The role changes. The matrix in `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 19, at line 1473 governs what it permits | Last owner would be demoted, so `E332` refuses | `share.role.changed`
Remove a person | Remove on a row | They lose access. Exports they already made are theirs | Removing yourself as the only owner, so `E332` refuses | `share.person.removed`
Add a second live collaborator on Free | A second person opens the document live | `E076`, which states the limit once and offers Pro | none | `share.collab.limited`
Set the link role | The link role selector | The link's role changes for everyone holding it | none | `share.link.role.changed`
Turn on the password | The password switch | Pro only. A password is asked for, hashed, and stored as a hash | Not on Pro, so `E333` offers the plan page | `share.link.password.set`
Set an expiry | The expiry selector | The link stops resolving after that moment. The document stays | Date in the past, so `E334` refuses | `share.link.expiry.set`
Copy the link | The copy control | The link goes to the clipboard | Clipboard refused, so `E335` shows the string to select | `share.link.copied`
Publish | The publish switch | The page becomes public at its slug, with the markdown twin beside it | At the page cap, so `E073` sends to S33 | `share.page.published`
Unpublish | The same switch | The URL goes dark. The document stays | none | `share.page.unpublished`

**The invite pays on sign-in, never on send.** Crediting on send would make the invite a faucet. The
credits land when the invited person signs in for the first time, and both sides get them then.

## States

state | when | what is shown | what the person can do
first-run | The first share on this account | The three blocks, with one line on what each means | Share, or close.
empty | Nobody added, no link made, not published | The input, the link block with the link not yet minted, the publish switch off | Add, mint, publish.
loading | The snapshot is being read | The blocks render disabled with their shapes in place | Wait.
partial | The directory lookup is still running | The address sits in the input with a quiet pending marker. No invite offered yet | Wait, or press add and take `E330`.
offline | No connection | The current state reads from the last snapshot. Every write is disabled with one reason | Read, copy an existing link, come back.
unauthorised | The viewer is not the owner and cannot share | The dialog does not open. The share icon is absent | Ask the owner.
over-cap | Published pages at the cap | The publish switch is off and `E073` explains | Unpublish another, or go to Pro.
AI-unavailable | n/a. Sharing calls no model | n/a | n/a.
conflict | The share state changed elsewhere under an open dialog | The dialog reloads the snapshot and says what changed. Nothing is merged | Reapply the change.
error | A write failed | The control returns to its previous position and names the failure | Retry.
degraded | The directory is up but slow | Adds still work. The invite block appears late rather than wrongly | Wait, or add anyway.

## Keyboard and focus

## Responsive

- **The dialog becomes a bottom sheet** at phone width, with a drag handle.
- **The role selector is dropped from the input row** and moves onto each person's row, because two
  controls beside a full-width email field do not fit.
- **The link box wraps to two lines**, with the copy control on its own row at thumb height.
- **Nothing is unavailable at phone width.** Adding people, minting a link, setting a password and
  publishing all work from a phone.

## Desktop differences

**None.** The Tauri build renders the same dialog. A share always goes through the server, because the
link, the password hash and the published page live there.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never add a person who has no frontmatter account.** Offer the invite instead.
- **Never grant invite credits on send.** They land at the invited person's first sign-in.
- **Never store a link password in the clear**, and never write it to a log.
- **Never let an expiry delete anything.** The link stops. The document stays.
- **Never say the live-collaborator limit more than once** in a session.
- **Never hard-code a cap on this screen.** Every number comes from the entitlement call, per
  `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 30, at line 1846.
- **Never publish without saying the page is public** and how many of the free pages that uses.
- **Never index a link-shared document.** Only a published page is public, and indexing is its own
  open decision.
- **Never show an owner a control that removes the last owner.**
