## Purpose

Two people edit one document at the same time and each can see where the other is.

## The number, and why it is one

**One live collaborator on Free. Several on Pro.** The founders confirmed it on 18 September at
`docs/mvp0/SCREEN-CHANGES-2026-09-18.md:305`, on cost grounds, and section 13 of the plan was changed
from three to one to match.

- The market says three. HackMD offers three invitees and AFFiNE three members, at
  `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 13, at line 1106.
- The reason we differ is stated on that same line: a live session is a Durable Object held open for
  as long as two people are in it, and that is the one free-tier cost that scales with time rather
  than with calls.
- **The plan's own cost work agrees.** Without the hibernation interface, live sessions are the
  largest line at 42.50 dollars and the free plan fails at 28 sessions a day, at
  `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 15, at line 1362.

## Entry and exit

Direction | How | Route or control
In | Opening a document a second person already has open | The workspace route, unchanged
In | A second person opening a document you have open | The same
In | An edit link, followed to the document | `/s/<token>`, then the workspace
Out | Closing the tab, which ends your part of the session | none
Out | The last person leaving, which discards the session state | none
Out | Being refused as a second collaborator on Free | `E190`, then S17 or S29
Out | A conflict that the session could not carry | S31

**There is no separate live route and no lobby.** The document is the session. A person who opens a
file somebody else has open is already in it.

**Presence is chrome, never content.** No avatar, cursor or highlight is ever written into the file.
The file holds markdown bytes and nothing about who was in the room.

## Data contract

What | Direction | Port or store | Shape | When
Session | read and write | `LiveSession.join(documentId)` | A shared document held in a Durable Object | While two or more people have the file open
Shared state | read and write | Yjs, inside that object | The shared document, and nothing else | For the life of the session
Presence | read and write | The same object | `{ accountId, name, colour, selection }` | On join, on move, on leave
Save | write | `ProjectFiles.splice` | A new version through the splice engine, per save | On each save, not on each keystroke
Session teardown | write | `LiveSession.leave` | The state is discarded when the last person leaves | On the last leave
Entitlement | read | `limitsFor(account)` | `limits.collab.live` | Before a second person is admitted

**None of this is built.** At `0af3c90` a search of `src/` and `src-tauri/` for a Yjs dependency, a
Durable Object or a presence type returns nothing. The whole screen is **specified, not built**.

**No CRDT state is ever persisted or merged into a file.** That is the plan's rule at
`docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 15, at line 1343, and it is the single exception the no-CRDT position allows. The
session is a shared buffer for as long as two people are in it. The record is the bytes on disk.

**The object's home is a best-effort hint.** Durable Objects offer jurisdictions for Europe and the
United States only, the apac hint is not a guarantee, and an object does not move after it is created,
at `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 15, at line 1349. So a session for two people in India may sit far from both, and
the round trip is a pilot measurement rather than a promise.

## Actions

Action | Trigger | What happens | Failure behaviour | Event id
Join | Opening a document somebody else has open | The session starts or is joined, and your avatar appears to them | Object unreachable, so `E191` and the document opens as a normal single-person edit | `live.session.joined`
Type | Any keystroke | The change lands in the shared state and appears to the other person | Transport drops, so `E192` and the editor falls back to local with a queue | `live.edit.applied`
See a cursor | The other person moving | Their caret and name are drawn at their position | Position no longer valid after a remote change, so the cursor is hidden rather than drawn wrongly | `live.cursor.moved`
See landed text | The other person typing | Their text is highlighted as it lands, and the highlight fades | none | `live.text.landed`
Save | The usual save | A new version is written through the splice engine, attributed to whoever saved | Splice ambiguous, so the engine refuses and `E193` says so | `live.save.written`
Be refused as a second guest on Free | A second person joining a Free document | `E190`, stated once, with the option to invite through Pro | none | `share.collab.limited`
Leave | Closing the tab | Your avatar goes. The session continues while somebody is in it | none | `live.session.left`
Last to leave | The last person closing | The session state is discarded. The file is the record | none | `live.session.ended`

**A save in a live session is still a splice.** Two people in a room does not license a whole-file
write. The engine locates the range and replaces it, or refuses.

## States

State | When | What is shown | What the person can do
first-run | The first live session on this account | The presence cluster, and one line saying what the highlight means | Edit, or dismiss.
empty | An empty document, two people in it | The session works. There is simply nothing on the page yet | Type.
loading | Joining | The document opens first. Presence appears when the session answers | Edit, and wait for presence.
partial | You are in, the other person is connecting | Your own editing works. Their avatar is quiet until they are in | Edit.
offline | You lose connection mid-session | `E192`. Your edits queue locally and the live pill goes | Keep typing. It syncs on reconnection, through the queue.
unauthorised | The document was unshared under you | `E194`, the session drops you, and your local copy stays readable | Export, or ask the owner.
over-cap | A second collaborator on a Free document | `E190`, once, with the Pro option | Invite through Pro, or take turns.
AI-unavailable | n/a. A live session calls no model | n/a | n/a.
conflict | A queued offline edit cannot be placed on reconnection | S31, both versions kept, nothing merged | Keep one, keep both, or send a proposed merge to the queue.
error | The session object failed | `E191`, and the document falls back to a normal single-person edit | Carry on. Nothing is lost.
degraded | Round trip is long enough to be felt | The live pill shows a slow marker. Editing is local-first and does not block | Carry on, or edit alone.

**Falling out of a session is never data loss.** The fallback is an ordinary edit against the same
file, because the file was always the record.

## Keyboard and focus

key | does
Everything the editor does | Unchanged. A live session adds no shortcut and takes none away
Escape | Dismisses the limit toast

**Focus on arrival is the editor**, exactly as it is when nobody else is in the document. Joining a
session must not move a person's caret.

**Presence is announced once, politely.** A screen reader hears that somebody joined, and does not
hear every keystroke they make, because a running commentary on another person's typing is unusable.

## Responsive

- **Presence moves into the phone header**, in the slot the mode segment usually holds, at
  `docs/mvp0/screens/gen.mjs:1362`.
- **The named cursor still draws**, because a phone editor still has a caret and the other person is
  still somewhere in the document.
- **The limit toast becomes a single line** above the bottom bar.
- **Nothing is unavailable at phone width.** A phone can be the second seat in a session.

## Desktop differences

- **A session survives a window losing focus**, because the connection belongs to the app rather than
  to a background tab the browser may throttle.
- **A file edited on disk by an agent while a session is open** does not enter the session. It enters
  the change queue on S20, which is the whole point of the queue.
- Everything else is identical.

## What this screen must never do

- **Never persist CRDT state**, and never merge it into a file.
- **Never write presence into the document.**
- **Never silently merge** what two people did. A save is a splice, and an unplaceable edit is a
  conflict.
- **Never block typing on the network.** The editor is local first, and the session is an addition.
- **Never state the free limit more than once in a session.**
- **Never draw a cursor at a position that is no longer valid.**
- **Never promise a round-trip time.** The object's region is a hint, and the number is a pilot
  measurement.
- **Never let a session be the only copy** of anything.
