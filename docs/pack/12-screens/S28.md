## Purpose

One place to change how the product behaves for me, on every device I sign in on.

## Entry and exit

How a person arrives | Route or control
The account avatar in the header, then Settings | `/settings`, opening on Account
The More item on the phone bottom bar, then Settings | `/settings`, opening on the section list
A deep link to one section | `/settings/<section>`, one of the ten slugs below
From S29 | The Plan and usage section is the tenth entry in the same nav, not a separate page
From S27 | The Appearance section holds the three-way theme choice

How they leave | What happens
The Settings tab close control | Back to the last document
Escape | Same, and only when no field holds a dirty value
Sign out, in Account | Session cleared, back to S01
A deep link out, such as Connections to GitHub | Leaves to the provider and returns to the same section

**A tab, not a modal.** `[O]` the shipped code disagrees. `src/modules/app-shell/presentation/SettingsModal.tsx`
is 82 lines and renders a modal with five toggles. The drawn screen is a full tab with a 220 px
nav. Counted with `wc -l` at `0af3c90`.

## Data contract

what | direction | port or store | shape | when
Account profile | read | `AccountReader.get()` | name, address, provider, created date | On opening Account
Account profile | write | `AccountWriter.rename(name)` | string | On blur, not on each keystroke
Preferences | read | `AccountPreferencesReader.all()` | a flat record of the rows above | On opening Settings, once
Preferences | write | `AccountPreferencesWriter.set(key, value)` | one key at a time | On each change, debounced once per second
Preferences, local cache | read and write | `localStorage`, key `sgnk-md-editor-settings` | the Zustand persist envelope | Read on mount, written on change
Connections | read | `ConnectionReader.list()` | provider, account, scope, connected date | On opening Connections
Entitlements | read | `LimitsReader.forAccount()` | the resolved limit set from S35 | On opening Plan and usage, and to disable a Pro-only row
Export bundle | write | `ExportWriter.requestAccountExport()` | a job id | On requesting an export in Data and export

**Two of these exist.** `[O]` `src/modules/editor/presentation/editor-settings.ts` is a Zustand store
persisted to `localStorage` under `sgnk-md-editor-settings`, holding five booleans: `vimMode`,
`lineNumbers`, `spellcheck` default true, `focusMode`, `aiGhostText` default false. Export exists as
`src/modules/vault/application/export-vault-zip.ts`. The account preference port does not exist.

**The key name is load-bearing.** `AGENTS.md:186` records that `sgnk-md-editor-settings` and its
siblings keep the legacy prefix on purpose, and that renaming one silently orphans a person's local
settings. It moves only behind a real migration.

## Actions

action | trigger | what happens | failure behaviour | event id
Open a section | Click a nav row, or land on `/settings/<slug>` | The section renders. The nav marks it | `E044` on an unknown slug, which falls back to Account | `settings.opened`
Change a toggle | Click the control | The value flips at once, the write is queued | The control stays where the person put it and a quiet retry runs. `E106` only after the retries are exhausted | `settings.changed`
Change a choice | Pick from the list | The same | The same | `settings.changed`
Rename the account | Blur the name field | The new name is written | `E106`, and the field returns to the stored name | `settings.changed`
Connect a provider | Click Connect in Connections | Leaves to the provider, returns to the same section | `E750` names which provider refused and why | `settings.connection.added`
Disconnect a provider | Click Disconnect | Asks once, then revokes at the provider and removes the row | `E099` if the provider refuses the revoke. The row stays and says so | `settings.connection.removed`
Export everything | Click in Data and export | A job starts. The file arrives by download when it is ready | `E105` names the part that could not be read, and the rest still exports | `account.export.requested`
Delete the account | Click in Account | A typed confirmation, then a 30-day window before removal | `E069` if a payment is in flight, which must settle first | `account.delete.requested`
Sign out | Click in Account, or the phone footer | The session ends. Local drafts stay on the device | None. Sign-out never fails loudly | `auth.signout`

## States

state | when | what is shown | what the person can do
first-run | A new account, nothing changed yet | Every row at its default. No empty panel anywhere | Change anything
empty | Connections with nothing connected | Each provider as an unconnected row, never a blank pane | Connect one
loading | Preferences not yet read | The local cache, so controls are live immediately, with the account read reconciling behind | Everything. Nothing is disabled while it loads
partial | Account read succeeded, connections failed | The sections that loaded, and one row in Connections saying the list could not be read | Retry that section alone
offline | No network | Every local preference works. Connections, Plan and usage and export are disabled with the reason on the row | Change local settings freely
unauthorised | The session expired while the tab was open | The sign-in prompt over the page, not a redirect that loses the pending change | Sign in and continue
over-cap | The account is over a cap from S33 | Nothing here is blocked. Settings is never gated | Everything. This screen is not metered
AI-unavailable | The chain is down, as in S32 | The AI section still opens and every switch still saves. One line notes the chain is down and points at S32 | Change AI settings, which take effect when the chain returns
conflict | Two devices change the same row inside the debounce window | Last write wins. The losing device updates in place with no prompt | Set it again
error | The preference read fails outright | The local cache serves, with one line saying the account copy could not be read | Work locally
degraded | Writes keep failing | A single persistent line in the page heading, not one per row | Keep working. Nothing is lost locally

## Keyboard and focus

- On arrival, focus goes to the current nav row, not to the first control, so a screen reader hears
  where it is before what it can change.
- Up and down move within the nav. Tab leaves the nav and enters the section.
- Every row is reachable by Tab in reading order. Space toggles a switch. Enter opens a choice.
- Escape closes Settings, and only when no field is mid-edit.
- **No shortcut opens Settings.** `UNVERIFIED:` `15-INTERACTION-AND-KEYBOARD.md` did not exist when
  this was written, so no conflict could be checked against the global map.

## Responsive

## What this screen must never do

- **Never rename a persistence key without a migration.** `AGENTS.md:186` lists the five legacy
  `sgnk-md` keys and says a rename silently orphans a person's drafts and settings.
- **Never turn on ghost text by default.** F049 and `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 5. It is the one
  switch that sends text without being asked, so it starts off and says so on its own row.
- **Never let a person set their own limit.** Caps are read here and written only on S35, through
  the one read path `limitsFor(account)` at `docs/mvp0/PRODUCT-PLAN.md` section 30. A second writer is a
  defect.
- **Never block on a save.** A control moves when it is clicked. The write follows.
- **Never hide what a switch sends.** Each AI row states what leaves the device and when.
- **Never show a captcha, a puzzle or a tour.** The founders' standing rule, and it applies here as
  much as at the front door.
- **Never require a restart.** The page heading promises it, so a setting that needs a reload is a
  defect rather than a caveat.
