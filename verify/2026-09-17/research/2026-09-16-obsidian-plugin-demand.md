# What Obsidian users install because the core does not ship it

Measured 16 September 2026 from the official registry:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-releases/master/community-plugin-stats.json`
and `community-plugins.json`. 7,638 plugins with stats, 147,920,815 downloads in total.
The top 60 plugins hold 87,544,012 downloads, 59.2 percent of all of them.

## The single strongest new signal

**Claudian**, "Embeds Claude Code/Codex and other local Agents as AI collaborators in your vault":
2,112,607 downloads, 15,335 GitHub stars, repository created 2025-12-05, pushed 2026-09-16.
About nine months from nothing to the thirteenth most-downloaded plugin in the registry.

**Copilot**, "Run AI agents such as Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode inside your vault":
1,900,500 downloads, 7,724 stars, created 2023-03-31, pushed 2026-09-16.

Together the agent plugins sit at 4,012,607 downloads. People want coding agents working
inside their markdown. That is frontmatter's thesis, measured in someone else's registry.

## Other recent fast risers

- TaskNotes, created 2025-05-18: 1,528,439 downloads.
- Notebook Navigator, created 2025-06-06: 971,308 downloads, "A better file browser and calendar inspired by Apple Notes, Bear, Evernote".

## Sync is routed around

Remotely Save (2,232,028; 8,106 stars), Self-hosted LiveSync (934,122; 12,338 stars) and Git
(3,143,722) together hold 6,309,872 downloads. A large share of Obsidian users will not pay
for Obsidian Sync, or want their own server.
