# Research report: Google Docs behaviours, Google Drive as an integration, bot checks

Every quote below is verbatim from a page opened by curl on 2026-09-16. Pages that failed are listed at the end.

## 1. What Google Docs actually does

### 1a. Twelve behaviours a small editor can copy

1. **A memorable URL creates a document.** Google Registry lists `docs.new` under "Start a new Google Doc, Sheet, or Slides presentation with this .new trick." (https://whats.new/). Google's own help also says "You can also create new documents from the URL docs.google.com/create." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618)
2. **A one-click blank document.** "In the top left, under "Start a new document," click Blank." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618)
3. **Every keystroke is saved, no save button.** "multiple people can work at the same time, you can see people's changes as they make them, and every change is saved automatically." (https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/9300503). The exact "Saving…" / "Saved to Drive" strings were not found on any Google page I opened; the nearest documented control is "At the top, next to the file title, click See document status." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6388102)
4. **"Last edit" is always visible, with a blue dot for unseen changes.** "At the top right, hover over Last edit to see who was the last person to update the file and when they last made changes." and "Tip: If there is a blue dot on the Last edit icon, that means someone updated the file since you last viewed it." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/190843)
5. **Suggesting mode: edits as proposals.** "You can suggest changes to a document without changing the original text. If the owner approves your suggestions, they replace the original text." and "You'll see your change in a new color. Anything you delete will be crossed out." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6033474)
6. **Bulk accept or reject.** "Click Tools Review suggested edits." then "Click Accept all or Reject all." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6033474)
7. **Named versions that will not be merged.** "You can create a named version to track your version history and make sure your versions aren't merged." and "You can add up to 40 named versions per document." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/190843)
8. **Restore or copy any earlier version.** "At the top, click Restore this version Restore." and "next to the version you want to copy, click More Make a copy." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/190843)
9. **Three roles, one link.** "Choose Anyone with the link." then "To decide what role people will have, select Viewer, Commenter, or Editor." Role table: "Viewer Yes No No Yes, by default. The owner can control it. No No" and "Commenter Yes Yes No Yes, by default." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822)
10. **Anonymous viewers are allowed and labelled.** "People who aren't signed in to a Google Account show up as anonymous animals in your file." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822); "People you didn't invite individually will show as anonymous animals when they're in the file." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494888)
11. **An "@" menu for inserting structured things.** "Type "@" anywhere in your document or click the "@" button on a blank line." and "To add a date chip, type: @today @tomorrow @yesterday @date" (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/10710316)
12. **Templates open as a copy, never the original.** "At the top right, click Template Gallery. Click the template you want to use. A copy of the template opens." and, from inside a document, "Click File New From a template." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/148833)

Markdown, for reference: "Go to Tools Preferences. Check or uncheck the box next to "Enable Markdown"." Typed syntax is limited: "With Markdown, you can format text to add: Italics Bold Strikethrough Links" plus "up to 6 different headings". Also "Right-click and select Paste from Markdown.", "Right-click and select Copy as Markdown.", "Click File Download. Select Markdown (.md)." and "Click File Open Upload. Select your Markdown file, this is uploaded and opens up automatically." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/12014036)

### 1b. Five things Google Docs does not do

1. **Markdown is not the record; it is an export.** "You can export the document as Markdown for use in another application. You'll download a Markdown file (.md)." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/12014036)
2. **No local files; offline is a browser cache with strict requirements.** "You can save the most recently opened documents on your computer." but "You must use the Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge browser." "Don't use private browsing." "Install and turn on Google Docs Offline Chrome extension." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6388102)
3. **History is gated on edit rights, and per-span attribution is a paid tier.** "To browse earlier versions of a file, you need permission to edit that file." and for "Show editors": "This feature is available to only Google Workspace Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise Standard, Enterprise Plus, and Education Plus customers." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/190843)
4. **Sharing leaks the owner's identity and has hard caps.** "When you share a link to a file, your name and email will be visible as the owner of that file." "You can share a single file with up to 600 individual email addresses." A file "can only be edited on up to 100 open tabs or devices." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/2494822)
5. **No agent hand-off.** No opened Google page mentions agents, kits, or handing a document to a tool; suggestions are a human loop: "The owner of the file will get an email about your suggestions and can decide whether to keep them." (https://support.google.com/docs/answer/6033474). Note: I did not find a page stating an account is required to edit; the pages state only that unsigned people show as anonymous animals.

## 2. Google Drive as an integration

**Scope classes** (https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/api-specific-auth):
- Non-sensitive: "The following Drive API scopes are recommended for most use cases:" including `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` described as "Create new Drive files, or modify existing files, that you open with an app or that the user shares with an app while using the Google Picker API or the app's file picker." Also `drive.appdata`, `drive.appfolder`, `drive.install`.
- Sensitive: only `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.apps.readonly`, "View apps authorized to access your Drive."
- Restricted: `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive`, "View and manage all your Drive files."; plus `drive.readonly`, `drive.activity`, `drive.activity.readonly`, `drive.meet.readonly`, `drive.metadata`, `drive.metadata.readonly`, `drive.scripts`.
- Definitions: "Non-sensitive : These scopes provide the smallest scope of authorization and only require basic OAuth App Verification." "Restricted : These scopes provide wide access to Google user data and require restricted scope OAuth App Verification." "If you store restricted scope data on servers (or transmit), then you must go through a security assessment." And: "Straightforward verification : Since drive.file is non-sensitive, it allows for a more streamlined verification process."

**Verification and CASA:**
- "Apps requesting access to restricted scopes must meet the additional requirement of secure data handling by submitting to an annual security assessment from a Google empanelled group of security assessors." (https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13464321)
- "we are leveraging the industry standard App Defense Alliance and its Cloud App Security Assessment framework (CASA)." "Applications are assigned to either the AL1 or AL2 assurance level." (https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13465431)
- Timelines: "Brand Verification ... 2-3 Business days", "Sensitive Scope Verification ... 10 Business days", "Restricted Scope Verification ... 6 weeks", with "* Please note that these estimates are not guaranteed and will vary based on developer responsiveness". Cost: "Google does not charge the developer any fees for security assessment." "The cost for such a service is agreed on between the developer and the assessor without any involvement from Google." (https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13463817)
- Before verification: "Personal Use apps: If the app is for your personal use ( fewer than 100 users) , you and your limited number of users can continue using the app without going through verification(users will be allowed to click through "unverified app" warning screens during sign-in)." (https://support.google.com/cloud/answer/13464323)

**Picker** (https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/picker/guides/overview): "The Google Picker provides a polished, "File Open" dialog for information stored in Google Drive." Scopes row: web apps "Flexible; can use drive.file , drive.readonly , etc."; desktop and mobile "Strict; only drive.file is permitted and cannot be combined with other scopes." Also "File upload alternative : Users can upload a file into Drive directly through the Google Picker." and "Note that the user must be signed in while accessing the Google Picker."

**Quotas and price** (https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/limits): "Per minute per project 1,000,000 quota units", "Per minute per user per project 325,000 quota units", "Per day per project 1 TB" egress, daily threshold "Per day per project 400,000,000 quota units". "Usage under this threshold doesn't incur extra charges and your Google Cloud account isn't billed. Full billing details will be shared later in 2026 with at least 90 days' notice before any changes take effect." Per method: files.get 5, files.list 100, files.download 200, files.update 50.

**Verdict from these pages:** yes. "Open from Google Drive" is the Picker (a web app may use `drive.file`) and "Save to Google Drive" is a create or update on a file the app opened or created, which is exactly what `drive.file` covers. The user sees Google sign-in, a consent screen for a non-sensitive scope (an "unverified app" warning and a 100-user cap until brand verification, 2 to 3 business days), then a "File Open" dialog. Files never opened through the app stay invisible to it. No CASA, no 6-week review, no API fee under the daily threshold.

## 3. Bot checks

**(a) The HackMD challenge is AWS WAF's CAPTCHA.** AWS's documentation describes its puzzle screenshot as: "A screen contains the title "Let's confirm you are human" and the text "Choose all the chairs". Below the text is a 3x3 grid of images ... At the bottom of the screen are options to load a different puzzle, toggle the information box into and out of view, toggle to an audio puzzle, and change the language. Also at the bottom is the button "Confirm"." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/waf/latest/developerguide/waf-captcha-puzzle-examples.html). Same title, same "Choose all the X" pattern, audio toggle (the headphones icon) and language control. Caveat: hackmd.io pages I fetched by curl returned 200 without a challenge, so I could not see the vendor script on HackMD itself; the attribution rests on the exact wording match with AWS's page. AWS also charges for it: "You will be charged per each CAPTCHA attempt and Challenge response" (https://aws.amazon.com/waf/pricing/; the figures are JS-rendered and were not readable).

**(b) Cloudflare Turnstile.** "It's a simple snippet of free code that eliminates CAPTCHAs." (https://www.cloudflare.com/application-services/products/turnstile/). Plan table: "Pricing Free", "Up to 20 widgets", "Unlimited challenges (traffic or verification requests) Yes", "10 hostnames per widget" (https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/plans/). Modes: "Managed (recommended): Automatically decides whether to show a checkbox based on visitor risk level." "Non-interactive : Visitors never need to interact with the widget." "Invisible : The widget is completely hidden from the visitor." (https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/). Detail: "When an interaction is required, the visitor will be prompted to select a box. There will be no images or text to decipher." and "As a condition of enabling invisible mode, you must reference Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum in your own privacy policy." (https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/concepts/widget/)

**(c) Firebase App Check and reCAPTCHA.** "App Check helps protect your app backends from abuse by preventing unauthorized clients from accessing your backend resources." Web provider: "reCAPTCHA Enterprise"; "reCAPTCHA Enterprise is no-cost for 10,000 assessments each month, and has a cost beyond that." (https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-check). Tiers: Essentials "Free up to 10,000 assessments per calendar month *"; Premium "0–10,000 assessments per calendar month: Free *", "10,001–100,000 assessments per month: $8.00 flat fee", "More than 100,000 assessments per month: $1.00 per 1,000 assessments"; "* The free 10,000 assessments are per organization. The limit aggregates use across all accounts and all sites." (https://cloud.google.com/recaptcha/docs/compare-tiers)

**(d) When a check is warranted, from these pages only.** AWS: "CAPTCHA is commonly used when a Block action would stop too many legitimate requests, but letting all traffic through would result in unacceptably high levels of unwanted requests, such as from bots." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/waf/latest/developerguide/waf-captcha-and-challenge.html). App Check frames it as backend abuse by "unauthorized clients". None of the pages names sign-up or AI-credit abuse specifically. Options that run with no visible puzzle: AWS WAF's Challenge action, "Runs a silent challenge ... The verification runs in the background without involving the end user."; Turnstile Non-interactive or Invisible; App Check attestation with reCAPTCHA Enterprise. Reading a public note, as HackMD did, is the case AWS itself steers away from a puzzle.
