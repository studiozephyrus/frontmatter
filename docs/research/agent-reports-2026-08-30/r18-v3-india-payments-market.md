### What must change in the record

1. **Delete "30-40%" entirely** from the section carrying claim 5. It has no independent source and it is the kind of number a due-diligence reader checks first. Replace with the RBI sentence, quoted and scoped: *UPI carried **83% of India's digital-payment volume in CY-2024**, up from 34% in CY-2019, while every other rail combined — RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, credit and debit cards — fell from 66% to **17%*** [fetched, RBI PSR H2-2024, read 2026-08-31]. State in the same sentence that this is all digital payments including P2P, **not** checkout share, so a later reader cannot re-inflate it.

2. **Rewrite claim 5's premise.** "International gateways without UPI" is false as a generalisation and must not survive into a pitch. The true and more useful statement: **Stripe offers UPI to accounts in 35 countries — and India is not one of them** — and **Paddle presents UPI with no India entity or merchant account required**. For an India-based founder selling globally, the constraint is the reverse of what the record implies: the international rails have UPI; the India-domiciled Stripe account does not.

3. **Add the UPI AutoPay mechanics to the pricing/billing section** — these are hard product constraints for ₹299/₹599 and are now [fetched] from two independent vendor docs: per-transaction ceiling **₹100,000**; **recurring ceiling ₹15,000 per renewal**; an e-mandate authorises **a fixed amount**, so **an upgrade cannot be charged mid-cycle** (it fails and the subscription goes `past_due` until the customer authorises a new mandate) while a downgrade works automatically; **one-off charges against a UPI subscription return `payment_declined`**; and **renewals require a pre-debit notification with a mandatory ≥24-hour bank wait** (NPCI rule). This corroborates the ₹15,000 architectural constant already in the record and adds three constraints it does not yet carry — the proration mode must be next-billing-period, upsells cannot be immediate, and renewal-day revenue lands a day late.

4. **Fix claim 7's noun and its number.** "21.9M India GitHub **contributors**" → **"26.73M India GitHub developer accounts (Innovation Graph, 2026 Q1)"**. If the record wants a contributor number, the only sourced one is **~5M (12.5%), and it is specific to generative-AI repositories**. Keep "+5.2M in a year" only if the sentence also carries its vintage (Octoverse 2025, ~Aug-2025 cutoff); the current-window figure is **+7,469,415 (+38.8%), 2026 Q1 vs 2025 Q1** [derived].

5. **Qualify claim 7's app-spend figure.** "+35% YoY consumer app spend" → **"+35% YoY India in-app-purchase revenue, $345M in Q2 2026 (Sensor Tower; IAP on Google Play and the App Store only — excludes advertising and third-party Android stores)."** The non-gaming subset, which is the one that matters for this product, grew faster: **~$240M, +50% YoY**.

6. **Soften claim 8's absence clause and replace it with the evidence that does exist.** "Zero category-specific willingness-to-pay evidence" → **"No published India-specific pricing study for markdown or PKM tools was located (OpenAI's pricing pages returned 403 and the 2026 Stack Overflow survey 404, so two obvious checks are open). The adjacent evidence is positive: India's non-gaming IAP revenue grew ~50% YoY to ~$240M in Q2 2026, with generative-AI subscriptions leading monetisation — Claude ranked No.2 by revenue among GenAI apps in India while only No.5 by downloads. Obsidian, the category leader, publishes USD-only pricing with no INR tier, so the gap is an unpriced market, not an unproven one."**

7. **Upgrade claim 8's "greenfield" from rhetoric to a measurement, and put the numbers in the record.** Replace the assertion with: **India's `markdown` topic on GitHub carried 440 unique pushers in 2026 Q1 (US: 1,296), up from 104 in 2020 Q2; the `obsidian` topic has never cleared GitHub's 100-developer reporting threshold for India in any quarter since 2020 Q1, while it clears it for the US in 12 quarters** [measured, Innovation Graph `topics.csv`, read 2026-08-31]. This is the strongest sentence in the whole set because it is the only absence claim in the record that now has a falsifiable floor under it — and it should be re-run each quarter rather than restated.
